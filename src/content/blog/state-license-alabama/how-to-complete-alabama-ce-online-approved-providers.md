Online continuing education has become the standard for Alabama insurance producers. It's flexible, accessible, and — when done through ALDOI-approved providers — satisfies all your CE requirements efficiently. Understanding how online CE works in Alabama and what to look for in approved providers helps you complete requirements without stress.

Here's your guide to completing Alabama CE online through approved providers.

## Why Online CE Works Well for Alabama Producers

Online continuing education offers significant advantages:

Flexibility. Study during evenings, weekends, or whenever fits your schedule.

Self-paced learning. Move through material at your own speed. Spend more time on unfamiliar topics, less on ones you already know well.

No travel. Complete CE from home, office, or anywhere with internet access.

Immediate access. Start courses immediately after enrolling rather than waiting for scheduled classes.

Replayable content. Revisit sections you want to reinforce.

Lower cost. Online CE is typically less expensive than in-person instruction.

These benefits make online CE the practical choice for most Alabama agents balancing work, family, and continuing education requirements.

## ALDOI Approval of Online Courses

Alabama's Department of Insurance approves online courses the same way it approves classroom courses. To be considered approved:

The provider must be registered and approved by the ALDOI

Each individual course must be reviewed and approved

Course content must meet ALDOI standards for accuracy and relevance

Approval is typically tied to a specific license type and topic area

If a provider or course isn't ALDOI-approved, your completion doesn't count — even if the course content is excellent.

## How to Verify Provider Approval

Before enrolling in any Alabama CE course, verify:

ALDOI Approved Provider Status. Check the ALDOI's approved provider list at aldoi.gov or ask the provider directly for their approval status.

Specific Course Approval Number. Each approved course has an individual approval number. Request this before enrolling.

License Type Match. A course approved for P&C licensees won't count for Life and Health holders. Confirm the course applies to your specific license.

Current Approval. Approvals can expire or be withdrawn. Verify the course is currently approved.

Reporting Process. Approved providers report completions electronically to the ALDOI. Confirm the provider's reporting process.

## Alabama's Course Monitoring Requirements

Alabama has specific requirements for course monitoring:

Self-study examinations must be closed-book and monitored by a disinterested third party (someone with no family or financial relationship with you) who certifies the completion was done without outside assistance.

Online course platforms must include features that verify student engagement and completion.

Final exam requirements typically require a 70% or better score to receive credit for the course.

These requirements ensure CE completions represent genuine learning rather than just clicking through content.

## Tips for Successful Online CE

Set aside dedicated time. Don't try to complete CE in small fragments between other tasks. Block dedicated time for focused learning.

Choose engaging content. Quality courses keep you engaged; poorly designed ones drag. Research providers before enrolling.

Take notes. Writing down key points reinforces learning.

Don't just click through. CE is most valuable when you actually engage with content. Skimming without absorbing wastes your time.

Complete courses in one or two sittings when possible. Breaking up courses extensively reduces retention.

Review before the final exam. A few minutes of review before the final exam can make the difference between passing and failing.

Save your certificates. Keep digital and printed copies as backup documentation.

## Pricing for Alabama Online CE

Alabama online CE pricing varies by provider and course length:

Individual courses typically range from $10-$30 per CE hour, depending on the provider and topic.

CE packages covering full 24-hour requirements often cost $60-$200 for the complete package.

Specialty training (LTC, annuity, flood) typically costs $25-$100 per course.

Bundle discounts are common for full CE packages and may offer better value than purchasing individual courses.

Remember Alabama's $1.00 per credit hour reporting fee — this may be included in course pricing or charged separately.

## Warning Signs of Non-Approved or Low-Quality Providers

Watch out for:

Providers who can't confirm ALDOI approval

Prices dramatically lower than other providers without explanation

Vague language like "may count toward CE" rather than confirming approval

No ALDOI approval numbers visible on course materials

No physical business address or verifiable business presence

No customer support access

Sketchy reviews or no established track record

Reputable providers are transparent about their approval status. Red flags suggest courses that won't count toward your renewal.

## What If a Course Doesn't Report to the ALDOI?

If you've completed a course but don't see the hours on your ALDOI transcript within a week:

Step 1 — Contact the course provider. Most reporting issues are resolved at the provider level. Providers can verify they transmitted and retransmit if needed.

Step 2 — Keep your completion certificate. Every approved course issues a certificate. Save it as backup documentation.

Step 3 — Contact the ALDOI. If the provider reports correctly but the ALDOI still doesn't show hours, contact the ALDOI directly with your certificate:

Phone: 334-241-4126

Email: ProducerLicensing@insurance.alabama.gov

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- Are online CE courses accepted in Alabama? Yes. The ALDOI accepts approved online courses, and most Alabama producers complete CE online.
- How do I know if an online provider is ALDOI-approved? Check the ALDOI's approved provider list at aldoi.gov, or ask the provider directly for their approval number. Reputable providers display this information transparently.
- How fast do online CE hours appear on my ALDOI transcript? Most approved providers report within 1 business day. Hours typically appear on your transcript within 1-3 business days.
- Can I complete all 24 hours through one provider? Yes. Many providers offer complete 24-hour CE packages. However, you can't repeat the same course within a renewal period for credit.
- What happens if I start a course but don't finish it? Most providers give you a specific access period (often 90 days) to complete courses. If you don't finish within that window, you may need to re-enroll.
