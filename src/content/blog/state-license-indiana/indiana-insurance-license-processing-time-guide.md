Indiana's licensing timeline from starting PLE to holding an active producer license typically runs 4 to 8 weeks. The 6-month PLE certificate validity creates the outer boundary, but most organized candidates complete the process in 4-6 weeks. The 5-7 business day IDOI processing is fast — the primary variables are how quickly candidates complete PLE, schedule and pass the Pearson VUE exam, and get Gemalto fingerprinting processed. The 180-day temporary license provides a meaningful bridge for candidates who need to begin working before full licensing. Here's the realistic breakdown.

## Timeline Overview

## Stage 1: PLE Completion

Indiana's 20-hour PLE requirement (per line) is self-paced through IDOI-approved providers. Most candidates complete a single-line 20-hour course in 1-2 weeks at 2-4 hours per day. Combined LA&H preparation (40 hours) takes approximately 2 weeks at that pace.

Critical: The 6-month clock starts from the date on the Certificate of Completion. Complete PLE and schedule the Pearson VUE exam immediately after completion.

## Stage 2: Pearson VUE Exam + Fingerprinting

Schedule the Pearson VUE exam within 1-2 weeks of PLE completion. Simultaneously, schedule Gemalto fingerprinting — both can be completed in the same week.

Exam day: Arrive 30 minutes early; bring photo ID + PLE Certificate of Completion. Results immediate.

If using OnVUE: Only one online attempt — prepare thoroughly.

Fingerprinting: Gemalto processes and submits fingerprint results to IDOI. Allow 2-5 days for processing and transmission.

## Stage 3: IDOI Processing — 5-7 Business Days

Submit Sircon or NIPR application with $40 fee after both exam and fingerprinting are complete. IDOI processes within 5-7 business days; candidate receives email notification.

Check status: Through Sircon or NIPR application tracking.

## What Slows Applications Down

Missing fingerprint evidence. IDOI requires evidence of completed background check before processing. Ensure Gemalto submits results to IDOI before applying.

Background disclosure documentation. Affirmative background responses require documentation.

Missing PLE certificate with application. Ensure PLE completion is properly reported to IDOI.

6-month clock expiration. If PLE certificate expires before application is submitted, must retake PLE.

Work authorization documentation. Non-US citizens must email documentation to [email protected]; missing documentation delays processing.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- How long does Indiana IDOI processing take? 5-7 business days for clean applications — approximately 1.5 calendar weeks. Most clean IDOI applications are processed within this window; the clock starts when IDOI receives a complete application including background check evidence.
- What is the fastest path from PLE to active license? Complete PLE → schedule Pearson VUE exam for next available date (1-3 days) → pass exam → complete Gemalto fingerprinting same week → submit Sircon/NIPR application immediately → wait 5-7 business days. Minimum total from exam date: approximately 10-12 business days.
- Can I start the fingerprinting process before I pass the exam? Yes — schedule Gemalto fingerprinting and the Pearson VUE exam simultaneously. If you complete fingerprinting and then pass the exam, you can submit the application immediately. If you fail the exam, fingerprinting results remain valid while you retake.
- Can I expedite IDOI processing? IDOI standard processing is 5-7 business days without expedited options. For time-sensitive situations, the 180-day temporary license (with IDOI approval) allows immediate activity while the full application processes. Contact [email protected] for temporary license procedures.
- What happens if I exceed the 6-month PLE validity window? If the 6-month Certificate of Completion validity expires before the license is issued (because you haven't submitted the application, or fingerprinting or processing delays pushed you past the window), you must retake PLE — including the new PLE final exam. The Pearson VUE exam score may or may not be separately valid; contact IDOI for current policy on exam score validity after PLE expiration.
