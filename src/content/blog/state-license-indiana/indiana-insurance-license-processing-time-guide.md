Indiana's licensing timeline from starting PLE to holding an active producer license typically runs 2 to 4 weeks. The 6-month PLE certificate validity creates a generous outer boundary, but most organized candidates move much faster. The 5-7 business day IDOI processing is fast — the primary variables are how quickly candidates complete PLE and schedule and pass the Pearson VUE exam (Indiana doesn't require fingerprinting, so there's no separate background-check appointment to schedule). The 180-day temporary license provides a meaningful bridge for candidates who need to begin working before full licensing. Here's the realistic breakdown.

## Timeline Overview

## Stage 1: PLE Completion

Indiana's 20-hour PLE requirement (per line) is self-paced through IDOI-approved providers. Most candidates complete a single-line 20-hour course in 1-2 weeks at 2-4 hours per day. Combined LA&H preparation (40 hours) takes approximately 2 weeks at that pace.

Critical: The 6-month clock starts from the date on the Certificate of Completion. Complete PLE and schedule the Pearson VUE exam immediately after completion.

## Stage 2: Pearson VUE Exam

Schedule the Pearson VUE exam within 1-2 weeks of PLE completion — most candidates can get a testing slot within a few days of requesting one.

Exam day: Arrive 30 minutes early; bring photo ID + PLE Certificate of Completion. Results immediate.

If using OnVUE: Only one online attempt — prepare thoroughly.

Indiana does not require fingerprinting or a separate background-check appointment — the background disclosure happens on the application itself, so there's no extra step to schedule between the exam and applying.

## Stage 3: IDOI Processing — 5-7 Business Days

Submit Sircon or NIPR application with $40 fee after passing the exam. IDOI processes within 5-7 business days; candidate receives email notification.

Check status: Through Sircon or NIPR application tracking.

## What Slows Applications Down

Background disclosure documentation. Affirmative background responses (prior administrative actions, criminal history, or license denials/revocations) require supporting documentation to be submitted with the application.

Missing PLE certificate with application. Ensure PLE completion is properly reported to IDOI.

6-month clock expiration. If PLE certificate expires before application is submitted, must retake PLE.

Work authorization documentation. Non-US citizens must email documentation to [email protected]; missing documentation delays processing.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- How long does Indiana IDOI processing take? 5-7 business days for clean applications — approximately 1.5 calendar weeks. Most clean IDOI applications are processed within this window; the clock starts when IDOI receives a complete application including background disclosure information.
- What is the fastest path from PLE to active license? Complete PLE → schedule Pearson VUE exam for next available date (1-3 days) → pass exam → submit Sircon/NIPR application immediately → wait 5-7 business days. Minimum total from exam date: approximately 5-7 business days, or roughly 2-3 weeks total from the start of PLE for an efficient candidate.
- Do I need to complete a background check step separately from the application? No — Indiana does not require fingerprinting. Background disclosure is handled through self-disclosure questions on the NIPR application itself, so there's no separate appointment or vendor step to schedule.
- Can I expedite IDOI processing? IDOI standard processing is 5-7 business days without expedited options. For time-sensitive situations, the 180-day temporary license (with IDOI approval) allows immediate activity while the full application processes. Contact [email protected] for temporary license procedures.
- What happens if I exceed the 6-month PLE validity window? If the 6-month Certificate of Completion validity expires before the license is issued (because you haven't submitted the application, or processing delays pushed you past the window), you must retake PLE — including the new PLE final exam. The Pearson VUE exam score may or may not be separately valid; contact IDOI for current policy on exam score validity after PLE expiration.
