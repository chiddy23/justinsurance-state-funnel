Wisconsin's license application process follows a clear five-step sequence: OCI-approved PLE (20 hours/line), Fieldprint fingerprinting ($36), PSI exam ($75/exam), NIPR application ($75/line), and 1-3 business day processing. The 180-day score validity creates the outer timing constraint. No combined exams are available, making each line a separate investment of PLE time and exam fees. Wisconsin's $75 application fee per line of authority makes full multi-line licensing (Life + Health + Property + Casualty) cost $300+ in application fees alone — but Wisconsin's 1-3 business day processing is the fastest of all comparison states. Here's the precise walkthrough.

## The WI Licensing Sequence

Five-step sequence with 180-day score window:

Complete 20-hour PLE per line (8 hrs shared + 12 hrs line-specific) → Certificate of Completion → 180-day clock starts upon passing PSI exam

Complete Fieldprint fingerprinting ($36) — can do before or after exam

Schedule and pass PSI exam ($75/line) — bring Certificate of Completion + ID

Wait 48-72 hours after passing → submit NIPR application ($75/line + $5.60)

Wait 1-3 business days for OCI processing → license issued

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

STEP 1: Choose Your Line(s) of Authority

Lines with PLE structure and exam format:

Life: 8 hrs (common) + 12 hrs (Life-specific) = 20 hrs; 100 questions; 2 hours; $75

Accident & Health: 8 hrs + 12 hrs = 20 hrs; 100 questions; 2 hours; $75

Property: 8 hrs + 12 hrs = 20 hrs; 100 questions; 2 hours; $75

Casualty: 8 hrs + 12 hrs = 20 hrs; 100 questions; 2 hours; $75

Personal Lines: 8 hrs + 12 hrs = 20 hrs; 100 questions; 2 hours; $75

Multi-line efficiency: The 8-hour common section (principles + WI laws + ethics) need not be repeated within 12 months. A candidate completing Life PLE first and then adding Health PLE within 12 months only needs 12 additional hours (not 20).

STEP 2: Complete Fieldprint Fingerprinting

Schedule at fieldprintwisconsin.com using code FPWIOCIInsurance.

Fee: $36.00

Bring two forms of signature ID (primary must be government-issued with photo)

Fingerprints valid for 180 days

Can complete before or after PSI exam — but must be done before OCI issues license

STEP 3: Schedule and Pass PSI Exam

Go to test-takers.psiexams.com/wiins

Create account or log in

Select exam (first-time: "General and State Specific" — e.g., "22-01 Life, General and State Specific")

Pay $75 exam fee

Choose in-person PSI test center or PSI Bridge remote testing (requires Chrome; check compatibility at syscheck.bridge.psiexams.com)

Exam day: Arrive 30 minutes early; bring government ID + Certificate of Completion. Score displayed on screen immediately.

If fail: no mandatory wait — immediately reschedule and pay another $75 exam fee.

STEP 4: Submit NIPR Application (After 48-72 Hours)

After passing exam:

Wait 48-72 hours for results to load in OCI system

Go to nipr.com

Complete Intermediary (Producer) Individual application

Pay $75 per line + $5.60 NIPR transaction fee

Answer all background disclosure questions accurately

Submit documentation for any affirmative background responses

Apply within 180 days of passing exam.

STEP 5: Wait for OCI Processing

Processing: 1-3 business days — fastest of all comparison states.

After 90 days from application submission, any required documentation not yet received causes application to expire.

## Application Fee Resolution: $75 Per Line Is Correct

Multiple sources disagreed on Wisconsin's application fee. The OCI official fee schedule confirms: $75 per line of authority for initial applications for lines requiring exams (Life, A&H, Property, Casualty, Personal Lines). The NIPR Wisconsin resident licensing page also confirms $75/line. Earlier sources citing "$10 per major line" appear to reflect an outdated fee structure that has changed.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- Can I submit the NIPR application before fingerprinting is complete? Yes — you can submit the NIPR application while fingerprinting is being processed through Fieldprint. OCI reviews the background check as part of processing. However, OCI will not issue the license until both the application and fingerprint background check are complete. Complete Fieldprint as early as possible to avoid delays.
- What is the 90-day documentation deadline? After submitting the NIPR application, you have 90 days to submit any required additional documentation (for affirmative background responses, etc.) before the application expires. OCI will request specific documentation if needed — respond promptly to avoid expiration.
- How does the 12-month PLE sharing window work in practice? If you complete Life PLE and pass your Certification Exam on March 1, the 8-hour common section is valid for any additional line's PLE through February 28 of the following year. During that window, adding A&H only requires 12 additional hours of A&H-specific PLE (not 20). After the 12-month window, the 8-hour common section must be repeated for a new line.
- Is the Fieldprint appointment mandatory before the PSI exam? No — you can schedule the PSI exam before completing Fieldprint. However, OCI requires fingerprint background check results before issuing the license. Most candidates complete Fieldprint concurrently with PSI exam preparation to minimize overall timeline.
- Can I apply for multiple lines of authority on the same NIPR application? Yes — if you have passed exams for multiple lines (Life and A&H, for example), you can submit a single NIPR application covering both lines. Each line still requires the $75 fee, but a single application submission covers multiple lines simultaneously. This is different from Wisconsin's exam structure (no combined exams) — you take separate exams but can apply for multiple lines at once.
