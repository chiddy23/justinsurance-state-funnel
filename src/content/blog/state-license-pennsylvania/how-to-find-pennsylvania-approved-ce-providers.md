Choosing a Pennsylvania PID-approved CE provider is essential — coursework completed through unapproved providers cannot be applied to your Pennsylvania CE requirement. With Pennsylvania's increased CE topic requirements (3-hour Ethics and 2-hour Flood Insurance effective April 29, 2025) and substantial total hour requirement (24 hours), finding quality approved providers requires knowing what to look for and where to verify approval.

Here's how to find PID-approved CE providers in Pennsylvania.

## Why Provider Approval Matters

The Pennsylvania Insurance Department maintains strict approval requirements for CE providers. Only courses from PID-approved providers count toward your Pennsylvania CE requirement. The approval process ensures:

Course content meets Pennsylvania standards

Providers comply with PID reporting requirements

Materials are current and accurate

Course delivery meets quality standards

If a provider isn't PID-approved, taking their course doesn't count toward your CE requirement — even if the content is excellent.

## Where to Find the Official Provider List

You can find Pennsylvania PID-approved CE providers and courses through:

Pennsylvania Insurance Department Website

The PID website (insurance.pa.gov) provides:

Approved provider lookups

Approved course searches

Course approval numbers

CE program announcements

Recent regulatory updates affecting CE

Sircon (sircon.com/pennsylvania)

Sircon provides additional licensing tools and CE verification for Pennsylvania producers including:

CE transcript access

Course approval verification

Provider lookups

License renewal services

National Insurance Producer Registry (NIPR)

NIPR provides various producer services including some CE-related functions.

## Key Information to Verify Before Enrolling

When evaluating a CE provider:

PID approval verification. Course must be specifically Pennsylvania-approved.

Course type designation. Verify whether the course satisfies:

General CE

Ethics (3-hour requirement)

Flood Insurance (2-hour requirement for P&C/Personal Lines)

LTC training (8-hour initial or 4-hour ongoing)

Annuity Best Interest (4-hour one-time)

NFIP Flood Insurance Certification (3-hour one-time for sales)

Hour count. Confirm hour count matches what you need.

Provider track record. Established providers with good reputations are safer choices.

Reporting practices. Provider should report completions promptly. Pennsylvania requires reporting within 10 days; quality providers report within 1-2 business days.

Customer support. Access to help when issues arise.

Pricing transparency. Compare full-package pricing including the $5 per course state reporting fee.

## Course Format Options

Pennsylvania accepts CE in multiple formats with significant flexibility:

Online Self-Study. Most popular format. Self-paced with online materials, quizzes, and final exam. No proctor required.

Webinars. Live or recorded webinars with attendance verification.

Classroom Instruction. In-person classes at provider locations.

Self-Study with Books. Traditional book-based self-study with online final exam.

Teleconference. Phone or video-based instruction.

Combination. Pennsylvania allows any combination of these methods. There's no minimum classroom or webinar requirement — agents can complete all 24 hours through online self-study.

## No Proctor Required (Pennsylvania Distinctive)

Pennsylvania eliminated the proctor requirement for CE final exams (following COVID-19 era changes). This is distinctive — many states require proctored final exams for online CE.

Practical implications:

Online CE is more convenient than in many states

No need to schedule proctor for final exam

Less administrative burden

Easier compliance for working professionals

This proctor elimination makes Pennsylvania one of the more convenient states for online CE.

## What to Provide Your CE Provider

When you enroll in a Pennsylvania CE course, you'll typically need to provide:

Your National Producer Number (NPN). This is the most critical piece of information — it's what providers use to report your completion.

Your Pennsylvania license number. Found on your license.

Your name as it appears on your license. Spelling variations cause reporting failures.

Contact information. Email for course materials and certificate delivery.

Double-check your NPN before enrolling. A wrong NPN means your completion gets reported to someone else's record.

## Reporting Timeline

After completing a CE course, expect:

Provider reporting: Pennsylvania requires reporting within 10 days. Quality providers report within 1-2 business days.

Pennsylvania PID processing: Up to several days after provider reports.

Total expectation: Allow 7-14 days for credits to appear on your transcript.

If credits don't appear after 14 days, contact your CE provider directly. Most reporting issues are resolved at the provider level.

## What If a Course Doesn't Report Correctly?

If you've completed a course but don't see hours on your transcript:

Step 1: Wait 10-14 days. Pennsylvania PID processing takes time.

Step 2: Contact your provider. Most issues resolve at provider level.

Step 3: Verify your information. Make sure your NPN and name match your license.

Step 4: Check your certificate. Save your completion certificate as backup documentation.

Step 5: Contact PID. If issues persist, contact:

Phone: (717) 787-3840

Email: ra-in-producer@pa.gov

Website: insurance.pa.gov
