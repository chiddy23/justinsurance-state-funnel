Walking into the South Carolina Life, Accident, and Health insurance exam without knowing what to expect is one of the most common reasons candidates fail. The exam has predictable structure, known content areas, and a clear passing threshold. Understanding these details lets you study strategically rather than broadly.

Here's what to expect on the South Carolina Life, Accident, and Health exam.

## Who Administers the Exam

South Carolina insurance licensing exams are administered by an approved testing provider (typically Pearson VUE or PSI — confirm the current administrator at registration time). You register and schedule through the approved testing provider and take the exam at an authorized South Carolina testing center.

## Where You'll Take the Exam

Testing centers are located in:

Columbia

Charleston

Greenville

Myrtle Beach

Florence

Other regional centers

Most candidates find appointments within 1-2 weeks of booking.

## Exam Structure

The South Carolina Life, Accident, and Health exam typically includes:

Approximately 150 scored questions (plus a small number of unscored pilot questions)

Approximately 2 hours 30 minutes to complete

70% passing score required

All questions are multiple choice with four answer options

You won't know which questions are pilot questions — they're mixed throughout. Treat every question as if it counts.

## Before the Exam: What to Bring

Two forms of valid ID. One must be government-issued with a photo. Common acceptable IDs include:

Driver's license

Passport

Military ID

Permanent resident card

The name on your IDs must match the name on your registration exactly.

Your exam registration confirmation. Bring a printed or digital copy.

Nothing else. Phones, notes, study materials, bags, watches, calculators, and electronic devices are not allowed in the testing room. Most testing centers provide secure lockers.

## Arrival and Check-In

Arrive at least 30 minutes early. This gives you time for check-in, security, and settling in without stress.

Check-in process:

Present your IDs

Sign in with testing center staff

Complete any required biometric verification

Store personal items in a locker

Receive scratch paper and pencil (or erasable board)

Be escorted to your testing station

Late arrivals may be denied entry and required to reschedule with additional fees.

## What the Testing Environment Is Like

Testing centers are quiet, controlled environments:

Individual testing stations with computers

Camera monitoring throughout

Staff observing through windows or monitors

Strict silence policy during testing

Other candidates taking various exams around you

Focus on your exam and tune out the environment.

## Taking the Exam

Navigation. The testing interface lets you:

Click through questions one at a time

Flag questions to revisit later

Review flagged questions before submitting

See your remaining time on screen

Time management. With 150 questions and 150 minutes, you have about 60 seconds per question. Most candidates finish with time to spare.

Strategy for hard questions. Flag questions you're unsure about and come back to them. Answer what you know confidently first, then revisit the harder ones with remaining time.

## During the Exam

Read every question carefully. South Carolina exam questions often include modifiers like "EXCEPT," "NOT," or "LEAST LIKELY." Missing these changes your answer entirely.

Don't overthink. Trust your preparation. Second-guessing correct answers is a common cause of missed questions.

Make educated guesses. Every question counts — there's no penalty for guessing. Never leave a question blank, especially as time runs out.

## After You Finish

Once you submit:

Your result appears immediately on screen. Pass or fail — no waiting.

You receive a printed score report at the testing center before you leave. Passed candidates receive confirmation; failed candidates receive a breakdown by content area.

Your score is reported electronically to the SCDOI. You don't need to submit results — they flow automatically.

## If You Pass

Congratulations! Next steps:

Complete fingerprinting if not already done

Submit your license application through NIPR or Sircon

Pay the application fee

Wait for SCDOI to process and issue your license

## If You Don't Pass

Don't panic. Many successful South Carolina agents failed their first attempt.

Review the score report to identify weak content areas

Wait at least 1-2 weeks before retaking — use the time for focused review

Re-register with the testing provider and schedule your retake

Pay the retake fee

Come back with stronger preparation in your weak areas

## Test-Day Tips That Help

Sleep well the night before. Cramming until 2 AM hurts more than helps.

Eat a real breakfast. Blood sugar affects focus.

Dress in layers. Testing centers can be cold or warm.

Arrive early, not on time. Rushing in spikes stress before the exam.

Use the bathroom before starting. Avoid distractions.

Breathe. If anxiety hits during the exam, pause for three deep breaths and reset.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- How many questions are on the South Carolina Life & Health exam? Approximately 150 scored questions plus unscored pilot questions. You have about 2 hours 30 minutes to complete all of them.
- What's the passing score? 70%. You need to answer at least 70% of scored questions correctly to pass.
- Can I leave and come back during the exam? Short bathroom breaks are typically allowed, but the clock keeps running.
- When do I find out if I passed? Immediately after submitting the exam. Your result appears on screen before you leave the testing center.
- How many times can I retake the exam if I fail? South Carolina doesn't cap attempts, but each retake requires a new registration and fee. Most candidates pass within 1-2 attempts with proper preparation.
