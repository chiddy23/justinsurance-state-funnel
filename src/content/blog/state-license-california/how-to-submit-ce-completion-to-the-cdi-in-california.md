Completing your CE hours is only half the job. If those hours aren't submitted to the California Department of Insurance properly, they don't count — and you could end up scrambling at renewal time or facing a license lapse. Fortunately, California's CE submission process is mostly automated. But knowing how it works protects you from surprises.

Here's exactly how CE completion gets submitted to CDI and what to verify on your end.

## The Provider Submits Your Hours, Not You

The most important thing to understand about California CE submission: you don't manually submit CE completions to CDI yourself. Your approved course provider does it.

Every CDI-approved CE provider is required to submit your course completion electronically to CDI after you finish. Submission typically happens within a specific timeframe — usually within a few weeks of completion. This automated reporting is a core requirement for any provider to maintain CDI approval.

As long as you take courses through legitimate CDI-approved providers, your hours should flow to CDI automatically.

## How to Verify Your Hours Were Submitted

Don't just assume it happened. Verify.

You can check your CE record through CDI's online producer portal. Log in with your license credentials and review your CE history. Every course you've taken through an approved provider should appear on your record within a few weeks of completion.

Check your CE status periodically throughout your renewal cycle:

- At the halfway point of your 2-year cycle — catch issues early
- At the 60-day mark before renewal — last chance to fix anything missing
- At the 30-day mark — confirm everything is in place
- Catching missing hours early gives you time to fix them without stress.

## What to Do If Hours Aren't Showing Up

If you completed a course but don't see it on your CDI record after 30 days:

Step 1 — Contact the course provider directly. Ask them to confirm they submitted your completion. Most reporting issues are resolved at this level. Providers can retransmit or correct submissions if there's been an error.

Step 2 — Keep your completion certificate. Every approved provider issues a certificate after course completion. Save it — digitally, printed, or both. This certificate is your proof if there's ever a dispute about whether you completed the course.

Step 3 — Contact CDI. If the provider confirms submission but CDI's record still shows nothing, contact CDI directly. Have your certificate ready along with the provider's information. CDI can investigate and correct their record when there's documentation to support it.

## Timing Matters

CE hours must be completed before your renewal deadline — not submitted, not reported, but actually completed. The submission itself happens after, but the work must be done in time.

Don't wait until the final week before renewal to complete your hours. Even with automated reporting, there's typically a lag between completion and CDI's record updating. Building in a buffer of at least 30 days before your deadline protects you from last-minute surprises.

Ideal timing:

- Complete most hours throughout the cycle
- Finish all required hours at least 60 days before renewal
- Verify CDI's record at least 30 days before renewal
- Submit renewal application once everything is confirmed

## Renewing Your License

When renewal time arrives, you submit your renewal application through:

- Sircon (sircon.com)
- NIPR (nipr.com)
- CDI's online producer portal

CDI confirms your CE compliance as part of the renewal review. If your hours are all in place and reported, renewal typically processes quickly. If there's a shortfall, the system flags it and renewal is delayed until the missing hours are addressed.

## What CDI's Record Shows

When you look at your CE record in CDI's portal, you'll typically see:

- Course names and dates
- Providers who submitted each course
- Hours earned per course
- Categories (Ethics, general, LTC-specific, annuity-specific)
- Total hours completed in your current cycle
- Status of specific required components (Ethics, LTC refresher if applicable, annuity training if applicable)

Take time to understand this breakdown. It helps you confirm that specific requirements — not just total hours — are satisfied.

## What If Your Provider Goes Out of Business?

This is rare but does happen. If a provider you took courses from goes out of business before submitting your completion:

- Contact CDI directly with your completion certificate
- CDI may accept documentation from the closed provider as proof of completion
- Be prepared to provide all documentation you have
- This is another reason to save completion certificates permanently — you never know when you might need them.

## Best Practices for CE Tracking

Take courses only from approved providers. Non-approved courses create reporting nightmares and don't count.

Save every certificate permanently. Digital copies in cloud storage plus printed originals in a folder.

Log in to CDI's portal at least 4 times per cycle. Don't rely solely on provider reporting.

Don't wait until the last month. Build CE completion into your routine throughout the cycle.

Maintain a personal CE log. A simple spreadsheet listing every course, date, provider, hours, and completion certificate location protects you against any reporting gap.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long does it take for completed CE to appear on my CDI record? Typically 7 to 30 days. Some providers report within days; others take a few weeks. Check CDI's portal periodically to track reporting.

2. Can I self-report CE completions to CDI? No. California's system requires that approved providers submit completions electronically. Self-reporting isn't part of the standard process.

3. Do I need to keep proof of my CE completions? Yes. Save every completion certificate indefinitely. They're your backup if there's ever a reporting discrepancy or if a provider's records become unavailable.

4. What if I see an error in CDI's record — wrong hours, wrong course? Contact the provider first to correct the submission. If the provider submitted correctly but CDI's record is wrong, contact CDI directly with documentation.

5. Does CDI notify me when my CE is complete or if anything is missing? CDI sends renewal notices with information about CE status, but the ultimate responsibility to verify is yours. Don't rely solely on notifications.
