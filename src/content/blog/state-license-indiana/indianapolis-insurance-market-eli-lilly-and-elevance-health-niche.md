## Eli Lilly and Company — Indianapolis's Pharma Giant

Eli Lilly and Company (Indianapolis) is one of the world's most significant pharmaceutical companies:

Fortune 500; revenue exceeding $34 billion (2023); one of the strongest-performing S&P 500 stocks over the past decade

Global pharmaceutical leader in insulin (discovered at U of T; Lilly first manufacturer; global insulin leader); oncology (Verzenio breast cancer; Jaypirca); immunology (Taltz, Olumiant); and Alzheimer's (Leqembi partnership; donanemab pipeline)

Indianapolis headquarters campus employs tens of thousands

Lilly's global success story — the company's stock performance has created significant wealth for long-term Lilly employees through RSU accumulation

Lilly employee profiles:

Research scientists and PhD researchers:

Salaries: $90,000-$200,000+ depending on level

RSU compensation at senior principal scientist level and above

Disability income need: protecting the ability to perform specialized research (own-occupation definition matters)

LTC planning: awareness of healthcare costs creates LTC receptivity

Program managers and directors:

Salaries: $130,000-$280,000

RSU and annual bonus compensation

Life insurance gap: Lilly's group life (typically 1-2x salary) may be insufficient for $200,000+ earners with mortgages, children's education, and wealth accumulation goals

Senior executives and C-suite:

Total compensation: $500,000-$5,000,000+

Complex executive benefits, deferred compensation, and concentrated stock positions

Executive-level life insurance (split dollar, key person), executive disability income, and estate planning-adjacent advisory needs

Building a Lilly-adjacent practice: Partner with Carmel, Zionsville, and north Indianapolis financial planners and CPAs who serve Lilly employees. Lilly's annual vesting cycles (RSU release dates) create predictable windows when employees have financial planning conversations — timing advisory outreach to post-vesting periods is a practical relationship strategy. Lilly's research community publishes scientific findings — attending Indiana Biosciences Research Institute events creates authentic science community engagement.

## Elevance Health — Health Insurance Giant in Indianapolis

Elevance Health (formerly Anthem, Inc.) (Indianapolis) is one of the largest health insurance companies in the United States:

Fortune 500; revenue exceeding $170 billion (2023)

Major Blue Cross Blue Shield affiliate; operates plans in 14+ states

Significant Indianapolis headquarters presence; thousands of Indiana employees

Business lines: commercial health insurance, Medicaid managed care, Medicare Advantage

Elevance employee profiles:

Actuaries and underwriters:

Salaries: $85,000-$200,000+

Deep health insurance knowledge creates a sophisticated advisory client base

IT and data professionals:

Salaries: $80,000-$180,000+

Growing Elevance technology organization in Indianapolis

Clinical and medical management:

Nurse case managers, medical directors, utilization review professionals

Medical professionals with deep healthcare awareness — LTC receptivity high

Directors, VPs, and C-suite:

$200,000-$1,000,000+ total compensation

Complex executive benefit, life, and annuity advisory needs

Advisory for Elevance employees: Elevance employees who understand health insurance at a professional level deserve expert individual insurance advisory — particularly for life insurance, disability income, and annuity planning where their employer-provided group benefits may be inadequate. The common mistake: assuming sophisticated clients don't need thorough explanation. Elevance professionals value thoroughness and accuracy, not assumptions.

Career transition advisory: Elevance Health has undergone significant restructuring since the Anthem rebrand (2022). Career transitions between health insurance companies, from carrier to employer, or from Elevance to startup health tech create COBRA, marketplace, and group benefit transition advisory moments.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- What makes Eli Lilly's recent performance significant for advisory? Eli Lilly's stock performance over the past 3-5 years (driven by GLP-1 medications Mounjaro and Zepbound plus oncology pipeline success) has created extraordinary wealth for long-tenure Lilly employees with significant RSU accumulation. Lilly employees who received RSUs 5-10 years ago have seen those positions grow dramatically — creating estate planning, insurance-funded gift strategies, and concentrated stock diversification advisory conversations that exceed typical pharmaceutical company contexts.
- Why is Elevance Health's Indianapolis presence distinctive for insurance advisory? Elevance Health is itself a health insurance company — but its employees still need individual life, disability, and annuity advisory. The sophisticated client dynamic at Elevance is distinctive: these professionals will quickly identify advisory that lacks depth or accuracy. Building authentic expertise in individual insurance products creates referral relationships within the Elevance community — one referral from an Elevance professional can lead to a community of similarly sophisticated colleagues.
- What is the Indiana Biosciences Research Institute and how does it create advisory opportunities? IBRI is a research institute that convenes Indiana's major life sciences organizations — Eli Lilly, Elevance Health, IU School of Medicine, Purdue University, and others — to advance biomedical research and innovation. Membership in IBRI-adjacent professional networks creates authentic relationships across the Indianapolis life sciences ecosystem — connecting producers to Lilly scientists, IU Medicine faculty, and health insurance executives in a professionally legitimate context.
- How does the Carmel, Indiana suburb fit into the Indianapolis professional market? Carmel (Hamilton County) is consistently ranked among the top 10 cities to live in the US — a planned, affluent suburban community 20 minutes north of Indianapolis. Many Eli Lilly executives, Elevance Health directors, and Indianapolis corporate professionals live in Carmel, Fishers, Zionsville, and surrounding Hamilton County communities. Carmel's median household income significantly exceeds Indiana's state average. Producers who serve the Carmel residential community reach the Indianapolis corporate professional community through their residential context.
- What is Salesforce's connection to Indianapolis? Salesforce purchased Indianapolis's tallest building (AT&T tower) in 2017 and rebranded it Salesforce Tower — anchoring a major Indianapolis presence for one of the world's leading CRM software companies. Salesforce employs technology and sales professionals in Indianapolis across its platform engineering, customer success, and enterprise sales functions. RSU compensation (standard for all Salesforce employees globally), variable income from sales commissions, and rapid career progression create consistent advisory demand.
