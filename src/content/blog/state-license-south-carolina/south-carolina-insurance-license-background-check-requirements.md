South Carolina is one of the states that requires fingerprint-based background checks for insurance producer licensure — a process that's been mandatory for all resident producer applicants since January 2017. Understanding how the background check process works helps you complete it efficiently and avoid licensing delays.

Here's a complete guide to South Carolina insurance license background check requirements.

## Why South Carolina Requires Fingerprint Background Checks

South Carolina implemented mandatory fingerprint-based background checks for insurance producer applicants effective January 1, 2017. The requirement reflects the SCDOI's commitment to:

Protecting consumers from licensed producers with disqualifying criminal histories

Maintaining industry integrity through thorough applicant screening

Aligning with national insurance regulatory best practices

Ensuring ongoing monitoring of licensed producers (new charges automatically reported once fingerprints are on file)

The requirement is established under South Carolina Code § 38-43-100(F)(a) and (b) and applies to all unlicensed applicants seeking resident producer licensure.

## What the Background Check Covers

South Carolina's fingerprint-based background check is comprehensive:

State Criminal Background Check. Conducted through the South Carolina Law Enforcement Division (SLED) — covers all criminal history within South Carolina.

Federal Criminal Background Check. Conducted through the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) — covers criminal history across all states and federal jurisdictions.

Ongoing Monitoring. Once your fingerprint records are on file with SCDOI, new criminal charges against licensed insurance producers are automatically reported to the SCDOI through the Rap Back program.

This dual state and federal approach is more thorough than systems used in some other states.

## Who Must Submit Fingerprints

All unlicensed resident producer applicants must submit fingerprints prior to licensure. This includes:

First-time insurance producer applicants in South Carolina

Producers transferring from other states (in most cases)

Applicants for additional license types where they don't already have current fingerprints on file

Applicants reinstating after license expiration beyond the grace period

## Who's Exempt From Re-Fingerprinting

Existing licensed producers are typically exempt from re-fingerprinting:

If you were fingerprinted at initial licensure (after January 1, 2017), no re-fingerprinting is required at renewal

If you were fingerprinted for your last compliance period, no re-fingerprinting is required as long as your license remains active

Once successfully processed, fingerprints remain on file with SCDOI

However, re-fingerprinting may be required if:

Your license has lapsed beyond reinstatement period (more than 6 months from expiration)

You're applying for a license type requiring fingerprints not previously submitted

SCDOI specifically requires updated fingerprints due to changes in records

## The Fingerprinting Process

South Carolina uses IdentoGO as its designated fingerprint vendor. The process:

Step 1: Submit Your License Application First

Apply through NIPR at nipr.com BEFORE scheduling your fingerprint appointment. Why? Because the SCDOI sends fingerprinting instructions and confirmation only after receiving your license application.

Step 2: Receive SCDOI Email

Within one business day of submitting your NIPR application, you'll receive an email from the SCDOI with instructions on scheduling your fingerprint appointment.

Step 3: Schedule With IdentoGO

Visit the IdentoGO website to schedule your fingerprint appointment. You'll need:

The service code from the SCDOI email: SC920124Z - DOI SC Producer Licensing/Renewal

Your government-issued photo ID

Payment method ($51.75 fingerprinting fee)

Step 4: Attend Your Appointment

Bring to your IdentoGO appointment:

Government-issued photo ID

Confirmation/scheduling information

Payment confirmation

The actual fingerprinting takes only a few minutes. IdentoGO uses electronic fingerprinting (LiveScan) — no ink involved.

Step 5: Receive Fingerprint Receipt

After your appointment, IdentoGO will provide a fingerprint receipt. Keep this receipt — it's your proof of completed fingerprinting.

Step 6: Send Receipt to SCDOI (If Required)

In some cases, you may need to send the fingerprint receipt to the SCDOI along with your National Producer Registry (NPN) number. Submission methods:

Mail: Attn: Individual Licensing, South Carolina Department of Insurance, Post Office Box 100105, Columbia, South Carolina 29202-3105

Email: (Use email address provided in your SCDOI confirmation)

Fax: (803) 737-6100

Step 7: SCDOI Processes Background Check

Once fingerprints are received, SCDOI processes the background check. Typical processing:

SLED state check: Usually completed within a few business days

FBI national check: May take longer (typically 1-3 weeks)

Combined results sent to SCDOI for review

Step 8: License Issuance

Once the background check is complete and reviewed, your license application is approved (assuming no disqualifying findings). You'll receive notification when your license is issued.

## Fingerprinting Fee

The fingerprinting fee is $51.75, paid directly to IdentoGO at the time of your appointment or scheduling. This fee covers:

Electronic fingerprinting service

State and federal background check processing

IdentoGO service fees

The fee is separate from your $25 license application fee, $59 exam fee, and any [prelicense](/south-carolina/prelicensing) course costs.

## Recent Process Changes

Effective August 22, 2024, South Carolina made changes to the fingerprinting process for Insurance Producers and Bail Bondsman licenses:

Updates to when fingerprint forms and applications must be submitted

Companies may not save and reuse ORI numbers/service codes

Public information containing fingerprint instructions must be removed immediately

These changes aim to maintain the security and integrity of the fingerprinting process. Always use current SCDOI guidance and only the service codes provided to you directly by the SCDOI.

## What Happens If Your Background Check Reveals Issues

If your background check reveals criminal history or other issues:

Disclosure on application is critical. South Carolina cross-references your application disclosure answers against background check results. Failing to disclose issues that appear in the background check creates serious problems including:

Application denial

Possible misrepresentation charges

Potential permanent disqualification

Documentation often required. For "yes" answers to background questions, submit supporting documents electronically via NIPR's Attachments Warehouse. Documents may also be submitted via mail, email, or fax to SCDOI.

Required documentation typically includes:

Court records for criminal matters

Documentation of prior regulatory actions

Explanation letters providing context

Evidence of rehabilitation

Other relevant documents

The SCDOI evaluates each case individually. Not all criminal history disqualifies you. Factors considered include:

Nature and severity of the offense

Time elapsed since the offense

Evidence of rehabilitation

Connection between offense and insurance industry

Pattern of conduct vs. isolated incident

You may be entitled to a hearing. If the SCDOI proposes denial based on background check results, you typically have rights to a hearing and review process.

## Common Background Check Issues

Outstanding criminal charges. Pending criminal matters typically need to be resolved before licensing.

Past felonies. Felonies may require detailed review and additional documentation.

Insurance industry violations. Prior violations of insurance laws or regulations face heightened scrutiny.

Financial fraud history. Insurance fraud, embezzlement, or similar financial crimes face particularly thorough review.

Crimes of dishonesty. Theft, fraud, forgery, and similar crimes face heightened review given insurance's trust-based nature.

Multiple offenses or patterns. Patterns of criminal conduct face thorough review.

Drug-related convictions. May require detailed review depending on circumstances.

License revocations or suspensions in other states. Prior insurance license issues in other jurisdictions face heightened review.

## Background Check Timing in Your Application

Understanding the timing helps:

Application submission to fingerprint instructions: ~1 business day Scheduling fingerprint appointment: Usually within 1-2 weeks of receiving instructions Actual fingerprinting appointment: A few minutes SLED check processing: A few business days FBI check processing: Typically 1-3 weeks Total fingerprinting and background check time: Approximately 2-4 weeks from application submission

Factor this timeline into your overall licensing plans.

## What If You're Already Licensed and Need to Re-Apply

If your South Carolina license has expired and is no longer eligible for late renewal (more than 6 months from expiration), you must apply as a new license applicant. This typically includes:

Retaking the appropriate licensing exam

Submitting a new application with full fee

Submitting new fingerprints (your previous fingerprints may not be acceptable for a new application)

Going through the full background check process again

## Pre-Need Producer Considerations

Pre-Need Insurance producers face additional requirements beyond standard background checks:

Must already hold an active Funeral Director license in South Carolina

Must pass the Life exam

Must submit a copy of the South Carolina Funeral Director's license

Must submit a letter of employment from a South Carolina licensed Funeral Home

## Bondsman License Considerations

Professional Bondsmen, Surety Bondsmen, and Runner Bondsmen face additional fingerprinting and documentation requirements:

Complete set of fingerprints required

Recent passport-size, full-face photograph required

State criminal records check required

Various additional documentation depending on bondsman type

## Tips for a Smooth Background Check Process

Apply through NIPR first. Don't try to schedule fingerprinting before you have SCDOI confirmation and instructions.

Use only the SCDOI-provided service code. Don't use generic codes you find online — use only the code specific to SCDOI Producer Licensing.

Schedule promptly. Once you have fingerprinting instructions, schedule promptly to avoid delays.

Bring proper identification. IdentoGO requires government-issued photo ID matching your application name exactly.

Keep your fingerprint receipt. Backup documentation in case follow-up is required.

Don't conduct insurance business until licensed. You cannot legally conduct insurance business in South Carolina until your license is officially issued and active.

Disclose honestly. Background check will reveal issues — disclosure is your friend, not your enemy.

Allow buffer time. Build extra time into your timeline for any background check delays.

## What to Do If You Have Background Concerns

If you have prior criminal history or other potential background concerns:

Disclose fully on application. Honest disclosure with proper documentation is your best path.

Submit explanation letters. Provide context and evidence of rehabilitation.

Submit court documentation. Provide actual court records for any criminal matters.

Consider attorney consultation. For complex situations, an attorney familiar with insurance licensing can help.

Request hearing if denied. You typically have rights to contest denial through hearing process.

Apply when timing is favorable. If you can wait until additional time has passed since past issues, that may help your application.

## Background Check vs. Continuous Monitoring

A unique South Carolina feature: once your fingerprints are on file with SCDOI, the system continuously monitors your criminal background.

What this means:

New criminal charges against you are automatically reported to SCDOI

SCDOI receives ongoing notifications about your criminal status

This continues throughout your active licensure

The system applies to all licensed insurance producers

Practical implications:

Any new criminal charges may affect your active license

The SCDOI knows about new charges quickly — you can't simply hope they won't notice

This creates strong incentive for ongoing legal compliance throughout your career

Any criminal matters that arise should be addressed promptly with the SCDOI

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- When did South Carolina start requiring fingerprints for insurance licensing? January 1, 2017. All resident producer applicants since that date must submit fingerprints as part of the licensing process.
- How much does fingerprinting cost in South Carolina? $51.75 paid directly to IdentoGO at the time of your fingerprint appointment.
- Do I have to be re-fingerprinted at renewal? No. Once your fingerprints are successfully submitted and processed, you do not need to be re-fingerprinted with future license renewals as long as your license remains active.
- How long does the background check take? Typically 2-4 weeks total — including SLED state check (a few business days) and FBI national check (typically 1-3 weeks). Plan for this timeline in your overall licensing process.
- What happens if my background check reveals issues? Issues don't automatically disqualify you. The SCDOI evaluates each case individually based on factors including offense severity, time elapsed, rehabilitation evidence, and relevance to insurance practice. Honest disclosure with supporting documentation is critical.
