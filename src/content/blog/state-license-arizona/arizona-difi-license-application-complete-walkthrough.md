The Arizona Department of Insurance and Financial Institutions oversees producer licensing — and understanding how DIFI's application process works, including several unique Arizona-specific features, helps you navigate efficiently. Arizona's process involves important decisions: combined vs. separate line exams, the precise fingerprinting sequence through Fieldprint and AZ DPS, and the relocating producer exemption that applies when moving to Arizona from another licensed state.

Here's a complete walkthrough of the Arizona DIFI insurance license application process.

## Who DIFI Is

The Arizona Department of Insurance and Financial Institutions (DIFI) — like Michigan's DIFS — is an integrated financial regulator overseeing insurance, banking, credit unions, and mortgage lending in Arizona. This broader scope gives DIFI comprehensive regulatory reach beyond insurance alone.

DIFI regulates:

Insurance producers, companies, and related entities

Banks and credit unions

Mortgage brokers and companies

Securities and various financial institutions

DIFI licensing authority (insurance):

Issues and renews producer licenses

Regulates insurance companies

Enforces A.R.S. Title 20 (Arizona Insurance Code)

Investigates consumer complaints

Disciplines licensees

DIFI Contact:

Address: 100 North 15th Avenue, Suite 261, Phoenix, AZ 85007-2630

Phone: 602-364-3100

Licensing email: [email protected]

Website: difi.az.gov

## Decision 1: Combined vs. Separate Line Applications

Arizona's application structure follows exam structure — you choose lines of authority:

Combined Life & Accident and Health:

One combined exam (150 questions, 2.5 hours)

One application and fee ($120)

Most common starting point for L&H-focused producers

Separate Life and A&H:

Two separate exams ($59 each = $118 total)

One application covering both lines

Allows focused preparation per line

May be preferable if stronger in one area

Combined Property and Casualty:

One combined exam (150 questions, 2.5 hours)

One application and fee ($120)

Standard path for P&C-focused producers

Separate Property and Casualty:

Two separate exams ($118 total)

Allows per-line focused preparation

All four major lines:

Two separate exams ($118 — one combined L&H, one combined P&C)

One application covering all four lines

Cost consideration: Unlike North Carolina (which charges $50 per line of authority), Arizona's $120 application fee covers all lines in a single application — so applying for multiple lines simultaneously doesn't increase the application fee.

## Decision 2: In-Person vs. Remote Testing

Arizona offers both in-person and remote exam options through PSI:

PSI Testing Centers (in-person):

Physical locations throughout Arizona (Phoenix, Tucson, Flagstaff, and others)

Controlled environment with in-person proctor

Preferred by many candidates for structure

PSI Bridge Remote Testing:

Take exam from home or office

Requires compatible computer, webcam, quiet private environment

Same time limits, scoring, and requirements as in-person

System compatibility check available at syscheck.bridge.psiexams.com before scheduling

Choosing format:

In-person: Better for candidates who prefer structured environment or lack compatible home setup

Remote: Better for candidates with long travel to testing centers or scheduling flexibility needs

## The Arizona Fingerprint Process — Multi-Step and Distinctive

Arizona's fingerprint process is more complex than most states — requiring accounts with two separate systems. This is one of the most common sources of confusion for new Arizona applicants.

Full sequence:

Step 1 — Pass your exam. Do not start fingerprinting until you have passed. All fees are non-refundable.

Step 2 — Create an account on the Arizona DPS Public Services Portal.

Visit the Arizona Department of Public Safety website

Create your account for the fingerprint process

Step 3 — Create an account on Fieldprint.

Fieldprint is Arizona's approved fingerprint vendor (the same vendor used across many Arizona state agencies)

Schedule your fingerprint appointment through Fieldprint

Step 4 — Attend your Fieldprint appointment.

Electronic (Livescan) fingerprinting — no ink cards

Bring required identification

Pay Fieldprint fee (~$20)

Fieldprint transmits fingerprints electronically

Step 5 — Receive your Fieldprint reference number.

After your appointment, Fieldprint provides a reference number starting with the letter "A"

Save this number — you'll need it for the next step

Step 6 — Complete and mail Form L-FPV.

Download Form L-FPV (Fingerprint Verification Form) from DIFI's website

Complete the form with your Fieldprint reference number

Mail to DIFI at: 100 North 15th Avenue, Suite 261, Phoenix, AZ 85007-2630

Step 7 — DIFI processes the FBI criminal history check.

$22 FBI processing fee (incorporated into the license application fee)

DIFI reviews results

Critical warning: Do not submit fingerprints through a generic IdentoGO page without a DIFI-specific service code. Generic submissions will not reach DIFI's processing queue and will not satisfy the requirement.

## Application Steps Through NIPR

After passing your exam and scheduling fingerprinting:

Step 1: Go to nipr.com and select "Apply for a New License."

Step 2: Select Arizona as the state and "Resident" as license type (for Arizona residents).

Step 3: Select "Individual."

Step 4: Select your lines of authority.

Step 5: Complete application information:

Legal name (must exactly match government-issued ID)

Date of birth

Social Security Number

Arizona residential address (P.O. boxes NOT accepted)

Contact information

Employment history

Step 6: Answer background disclosure questions:

Criminal history

Prior license actions in any state

Regulatory actions

Civil judgments

Financial history

Other professional license issues

Step 7: Upload supporting documentation for any "yes" disclosure answers through NIPR's Attachment Warehouse.

Step 8: Pay $120 license fee + $5.60 transaction fee.

Step 9: Submit application.

## What DIFI Reviews After Submission

Exam results. PSI electronically notifies DIFI within 48 hours of a passing exam.

Fingerprint background check. DIFI reviews FBI criminal history results from Fieldprint submission and Form L-FPV.

Disclosure answers. Cross-referenced with available records.

Supporting documentation. For any disclosure items.

Application completeness. Missing information triggers follow-up.

Form L-152 (Lawful Presence). Required unless fingerprints submitted electronically (which satisfies the requirement).

## The Relocating Producer Path

If you're moving TO Arizona from another state where you held an active resident license:

Exam exemption: If Arizona offers the exact same license type and line of authority you held in your prior state, you are exempt from Arizona's exam requirement.

Process:

Complete Arizona electronic fingerprinting through Fieldprint (within 90 days of receiving clearance from prior state)

Apply for Arizona resident producer license through NIPR

Complete Form L-CLR (Request for Letter of Clearance) and email to DIFI

Apply in your new home state (if leaving Arizona) within 90 days

Email copy of new resident license to DIFI within 30 days if you want to retain AZ non-resident license

90-day window: Arizona's relocation process has a 90-day window from receiving clearance from the prior state. Don't delay this process after relocating.

## Special Application Categories

Low income fee waiver: Applicants with family income at or below 200% of the federal poverty level may qualify for the $120 license fee to be waived. Download Form L-WAV from DIFI's website and submit with application.

Military/veteran applications: Active military spouses and veterans honorably discharged within two years may have fee exemptions. Contact DIFI for specifics.

Variable life/annuity producers: Must include a valid FINRA CRD number on the application in addition to standard requirements. Holds exemption from Arizona state exam requirement.

Non-resident applications: Processed through NIPR. Non-residents in good standing in home state exempt from Arizona exam. $120 license fee applies.

## After License Issuance

Arizona licenses are issued electronically. Check your DIFI account and email for notification.

License renewal: Biennial fee payment through NIPR ($120 per license class). Note: HB 2054 (June 2025) standardized all Arizona license renewal dates to December 31.

CE: Arizona's 4-year CE cycle requires 48 hours including 6 hours ethics. Do not confuse the biennial fee renewal with the CE cycle — they operate on different timelines.

Carrier appointments: Contact carriers for appointment paperwork. Arizona requires carrier appointment for producers to sell.

Assumed name/DBA: If operating under a name other than your legal name, complete Form L-193 (Certificate of Assumed Name) and file. Register the trade name with the Arizona Secretary of State to protect it.

## Common Arizona Application Mistakes

Fingerprinting before passing the exam. Fees are non-refundable. Pass first, fingerprint second.

Using the wrong fingerprint vendor. Fieldprint is Arizona's approved vendor. Generic IdentoGO submissions without DIFI's specific service code don't reach DIFI.

Forgetting Form L-FPV. The fingerprint verification form must be mailed to DIFI with your Fieldprint reference number. This step stalls many applications.

P.O. box as address. Arizona does not accept P.O. boxes as resident or business addresses.

Exceeding four exam attempts. The 4-attempt annual limit is hard. Adequate preparation before each attempt protects against this.

Waiting too long after passing. You have one year from passing to apply. Don't let this window lapse.

Confusing CE cycle with renewal cycle. Biennial fee renewal ≠ 4-year CE cycle. Both require attention on different timelines.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- What is DIFI and what does it regulate? The Arizona Department of Insurance and Financial Institutions regulates insurance, banking, credit unions, and mortgage lending in Arizona. For insurance producers, DIFI manages licensing, renewals, CE compliance, and disciplinary actions under A.R.S. Title 20.
- Why do I need accounts with both AZ DPS and Fieldprint? Arizona's electronic fingerprint process uses two platforms — AZ DPS for processing clearance and Fieldprint as the fingerprint collection vendor. Both accounts are required steps in Arizona's fingerprinting sequence. This two-platform process is distinctive to Arizona's system.
- What is Form L-FPV and why is it required? Form L-FPV is the Fingerprint Verification Form that must be mailed to DIFI along with your Fieldprint reference number after completing fingerprinting. Without this step, DIFI cannot match your fingerprint submission to your application, stalling the process.
- Can I apply for multiple lines of authority on one application? Yes. Arizona's $120 application fee covers all lines included in a single application — unlike some states that charge per-line fees. Include all desired lines when applying to avoid paying separate amendment fees later.
- What happens if I move out of Arizona after getting licensed? Complete Form L-CLR (Request for Letter of Clearance) and email to DIFI. Complete your new state's resident license application within 90 days of receiving Arizona clearance. To keep an Arizona non-resident license, email your new state license to DIFI within 30 days.
