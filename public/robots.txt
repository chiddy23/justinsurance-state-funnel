# Deny the bots that copy content to TRAIN models or feed AI datasets — they
# take the content and send no traffic back. This only binds POLITE bots; the
# rotating-proxy scraper (Botasaurus/Bright Data, spoofed browser UAs) ignores
# robots.txt entirely and is handled at the edge (Vercel Firewall ASN deny +

# owner decides the harvest isn't worth the referral exposure.
