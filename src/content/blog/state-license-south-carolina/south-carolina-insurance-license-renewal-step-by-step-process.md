Renewing your South Carolina insurance license is a straightforward online process — if you know the steps and you've met your [continuing education](/south-carolina/continuing-education) requirements. Miss the deadline or skip your CE, and renewal becomes more complicated. Understanding the process ahead of time keeps you compliant and active.

Here's exactly how to renew your South Carolina insurance license.

## South Carolina's Renewal Cycle

South Carolina insurance licenses renew on a biennial (2-year) cycle. Your license expires on the last day of your birth month, and which year you renew is keyed to your birth year — producers born in an even-numbered year renew in even-numbered years, and those born in an odd-numbered year renew in odd-numbered years. The SCDOI lists your exact expiration date in your online profile.

Renewal applies to all South Carolina insurance license types:

Life, Accident, and Health

Property and Casualty

Personal Lines

Adjuster licenses

Each has its own CE requirements that must be satisfied before renewal.

## Before You Renew: Complete Your CE

Before you can renew, you must have completed your continuing education requirements for the cycle. For most South Carolina insurance producers, this means:

24 hours of CE every 2-year cycle

3 hours must be in Ethics

A minimum of 8 hours in each line of authority you hold — so a producer licensed in both Life/Health and Property/Casualty must complete at least 8 Life/Health hours and 8 P&C hours, in addition to the 3 Ethics hours

Any hours beyond those minimums can be in approved elective insurance topics

Verify your CE completion through the SCDOI or Sircon before attempting to renew. If your CE isn't complete and reported, renewal will be rejected.

## Step 1: Check Your CE Status

Log into the SCDOI's online producer portal or use Sircon/NIPR to confirm:

Your current CE hour total for the active cycle

Whether your ethics requirement is satisfied

Whether any specialty CE (LTC, annuity training, flood) is current if applicable

Whether your CE is fully reported by your providers

If hours are missing or not yet reported, contact your CE provider first. Most reporting issues are resolved at the provider level.

## Step 2: Access the Renewal Portal

South Carolina insurance license renewal is typically completed through:

NIPR (nipr.com) — the National Insurance Producer Registry

Sircon (sircon.com)

SCDOI's online portal — through the state's insurance department website

All three serve as authorized portals. Use whichever you're comfortable with.

## Step 3: Log In and Start Your Renewal

You'll need:

Your South Carolina license number

Your National Producer Number (NPN)

Personal identifying information (date of birth, Social Security number)

Once logged in, select the renewal option for your license.

## Step 4: Complete the Renewal Application

The online renewal application will ask you to:

Confirm your current personal and business information

Update your address or contact information if needed

Answer disclosure questions about any new criminal history, regulatory actions, bankruptcies, or other reportable events since your last renewal

Confirm CE compliance

Answer disclosure questions honestly. Any new events since your last renewal must be disclosed. The SCDOI cross-references your answers against available records.

## Step 5: Pay the Renewal Fee

South Carolina's license renewal fee is paid online through the portal. Confirm current fee amounts through NIPR or the SCDOI at the time of renewal.

Payment is typically by credit card. You'll receive a confirmation email and receipt after submission.

## Step 6: Receive Confirmation

After the SCDOI processes your renewal:

You'll receive email confirmation

Your license status updates to "active" for the new 2-year cycle

Your license is active through the last day of your birth month two years out

For most straightforward renewals, processing is quick — often same-day or within a few business days.

## When to Renew

Don't wait until the deadline. Renew at least 30 days before your expiration date to give yourself buffer for any issues (CE reporting gaps, disclosure questions, processing delays).

Early renewal is fine. South Carolina generally allows renewal starting several weeks before the deadline. Early renewal doesn't shorten your new cycle.

## What Happens If You Miss the Renewal Deadline

If you don't renew on time:

Your license enters lapsed status

You cannot conduct insurance business until reinstated

You'll face late fees and potentially reinstatement fees

You may need to complete missing CE plus additional requirements

Grace period. South Carolina typically allows a short grace period during which you can renew with late fees without full reinstatement. After the grace period, full reinstatement procedures apply.

Extended non-compliance. South Carolina allows reinstatement only within 180 days (6 months) of your compliance deadline, by completing your full 24 CE hours and paying a $50 late CE compliance fee. On the 181st day the license and appointments are canceled — to become relicensed you must retake and pass the state exam, be re-fingerprinted, and submit a new application and license fee as a new licensee (including any required [prelicense](/south-carolina/prelicensing) education).

## Common Renewal Problems

Missing CE reporting. Your CE provider didn't submit completion to the SCDOI on time. Contact the provider to retransmit.

Missed ethics requirement. You completed 24 hours but didn't complete the required 3 hours of ethics content.

Incomplete specialty training. LTC, annuity, or flood training wasn't completed or reported.

Disclosure issues. New reportable events since last renewal need proper documentation.

Address or contact mismatches. Incorrect information in the SCDOI's system can cause delays.

## Keeping Your Next Renewal Smooth

Track your renewal date. Add it to your calendar, phone, and any business planning system you use.

Complete CE throughout the cycle. Don't wait until month 23 of your 24-month cycle to start.

Check SCDOI status quarterly. Log in and verify your CE is being reported correctly.

Use reputable CE providers. Approved providers report reliably.

Keep documentation. Save every CE completion certificate indefinitely.

## Renewal Responsibilities for Multi-State Producers

If you hold licenses in multiple states:

Each state has its own renewal cycle

Track each state's deadline separately

CE in your home state (South Carolina, if you're a resident) typically satisfies non-resident requirements in most other states

Verify specific state-by-state rules for any specialty training requirements

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- How often do I have to renew my South Carolina insurance license? Every 2 years. Your license expires on the last day of your birth month — in even-numbered years if you were born in an even year, odd-numbered years if you were born in an odd year. Check the SCDOI's portal for your exact date.
- Can I renew my license online? Yes. Renewal is completed online through NIPR, Sircon, or the SCDOI portal. Paper renewals are uncommon.
- What happens if I don't complete my CE before renewal? Renewal will be rejected. Complete your CE, have it reported to the SCDOI, then attempt renewal. This may cost you time and possibly late fees.
- Do I have to pay a renewal fee even if I didn't sell any insurance during the cycle? Yes. Renewal fees apply regardless of production. The fee maintains your license as active.
- Can someone else renew my license for me? You must complete the disclosure questions yourself honestly. Administrative staff can help navigate the portal, but you must verify and submit the application.
