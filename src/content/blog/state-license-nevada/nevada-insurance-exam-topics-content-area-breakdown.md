Walking into the Nevada insurance exam without knowing exactly which topics will be tested is a needless way to lose questions. Pearson VUE publishes the precise section-by-section question counts for every Nevada exam — right down to how many of the scored questions come from Nevada law — and most candidates never open the document. Working from those numbers lets you study strategically rather than broadly.

Here's a detailed look at Nevada insurance exam topics.

## Where to Find Official Content Outlines

Pearson VUE publishes two documents at pearsonvue.com/nv/insurance: the Nevada Insurance Licensing Candidate Handbook, and the separate Nevada Insurance Content Outlines (publication #122903, effective October 1, 2024). The content outlines are the authoritative source for what's tested.

The outlines don't give you vague weighting ranges. Every exam splits into a General Knowledge outline and a Nevada Specific outline, and each section carries an exact number of scored questions. Every exam also includes unscored pretest questions that Pearson VUE is trialing for future use — those don't count toward your score, and you can't tell which ones they are.

Here's the fact most candidates get wrong: the Nevada-specific section is big. It's 30 of the 80 scored questions on the Life exam, 38 of 88 on the Health exam, and 39 of 89 on the Casualty exam. If you study a generic national course and skim the state law material, you can miss the passing cut on that section alone.

Read the outlines before you start studying. Knowing how many questions each section actually carries lets you allocate study time by the numbers instead of by guesswork.

## Nevada Life Insurance Exam Topic Breakdown

The Nevada Life exam runs 2 hours and contains 90 questions: 80 scored plus 10 unscored pretest items. Here's how the official outline distributes those 80 scored questions.

Types of Policies — 15 questions

Traditional whole life, limited-pay, and single-premium

Universal, variable, variable universal, interest-sensitive, and indexed life

Term life — level, decreasing, return of premium, annually renewable, renewable and convertible features

Annuities — single and flexible premium, immediate and deferred, fixed, variable, and indexed, plus payout options

Joint life and survivorship life

Life Provisions, Riders, Options, and Exclusions — 15 questions

Riders: waiver of premium, guaranteed insurability, payor benefit, accidental death and dismemberment, long-term care, return of premium

Standard provisions, ownership rights, and beneficiary designations

Policy loans, withdrawals, partial surrenders, and nonforfeiture options

Dividends and dividend options, settlement options, accelerated death benefits

Exclusions — war, aviation, dangerous occupation

Completing the Application, Underwriting, and Delivering the Policy — 12 questions

Application procedures, replacement, and point-of-sale disclosures

USA PATRIOT Act / anti-money laundering and Gramm-Leach-Bliley privacy

Insurable interest, consumer reports, the Fair Credit Reporting Act, and risk classification

Contract law and the unique aspects of an insurance contract

Retirement and Other Insurance Concepts — 8 questions

Third-party ownership, life settlements, and group life

Qualified and nonqualified retirement plans

Needs analysis and suitability, personal and business

Social Security benefits and the tax treatment of life insurance and annuities

Nevada Specific — 30 questions, or 37.5% of the scored exam

Nevada statutes and regulations common to life, health, property, and casualty insurance (20 questions) — the Commissioner's powers, licensing, producer conduct, and unfair trade practices

Nevada statutes common to life and health insurance only (4 questions) — credit life and health, group life and health, advertising

Nevada statutes pertinent to life insurance only (6 questions) — marketing methods and practices, including replacement

Note what that means in practice: Nevada law is the single largest block on this exam, bigger than any general-knowledge section. Budget your study time accordingly, and don't skip [continuing education](/nevada/continuing-education) and licensing rules just because they feel like administrative trivia.

## Nevada Health Insurance Exam Topic Breakdown

The Nevada Accident & Health exam runs 2 hours 15 minutes and contains 98 questions: 88 scored plus 10 unscored pretest items. The official outline distributes the 88 scored questions like this.

Types of Policies — 16 questions

Disability income — individual, group, business overhead expense, business disability buyout, key employee

Accidental death and dismemberment

Medical expense insurance — basic hospital/medical/surgical, major medical, HMOs, PPOs, POS plans, FSAs, HDHPs with HSAs, HRAs

Medicare supplement policies

Group insurance, including how group differs from individual, and COBRA

Individual and group long-term care — eligibility and levels of care

Other policies — dental, vision, cancer, critical illness, worksite, hospital indemnity, short-term medical, accident

Policy Provisions, Clauses, and Riders — 15 questions

Mandatory and optional provisions — entire contract, time limit on certain defenses, grace period, reinstatement, notice of claim, proof of loss, payment of claims, change of beneficiary

Other provisions and clauses — free look, probationary and elimination periods, waiver of premium, preexisting conditions, coinsurance, deductibles, copayments, URC charges, benefit maximums

Riders — impairment/exclusion, guaranteed insurability, future increase option

Rights of renewability — noncancelable, cancelable, guaranteed renewable

Social Insurance — 6 questions

Medicare Parts A, B, C, and D

Medicaid

Social Security benefits

Other Insurance Concepts — 5 questions

Total, partial, recurrent, and residual disability

Coordination and nonduplication of benefits, occupational vs. non-occupational

Tax treatment of premiums and proceeds

Managed care, workers' compensation, subrogation, cost containment

Field Underwriting Procedures — 8 questions

Completing and submitting the application, initial premium and receipts

HIPAA privacy, the MIB report, and the Fair Credit Reporting Act

Policy delivery, explaining provisions and ratings, replacement

Contract law, insurable interest, warranties and representations

Nevada Specific — 38 questions, or 43.2% of the scored exam

Nevada statutes and codes common to life, health, property, and casualty insurance (20 questions)

Nevada statutes common to life and health insurance only (4 questions) — credit life and health, group life and health, advertising

Nevada statutes pertinent to health insurance only (14 questions) — mandatory policy clauses and provisions, and Nevada's health-specific consumer protections

More than four questions in ten on the Health exam come from the Nevada outline — only Casualty runs higher.

## Nevada Combined Life and Health Exam

The combined exam runs 3 hours 35 minutes and contains 159 questions: 144 scored plus 15 unscored pretest items. It is built from three outlines rather than two, and the general-knowledge halves are identical to the standalone exams:

Life General Knowledge — 50 questions, the same four sections described above

Accident & Health General Knowledge — 50 questions, the same five sections described above

Life/Health Nevada Specific — 44 questions, or 30.6% of the scored exam

That Nevada Specific outline is the combined version of the two state outlines: 20 questions on statutes common to all four major lines, 4 on statutes common to life and health only, 6 on life only, and 14 on health only.

One useful consequence of taking the combined exam: the Nevada-law share drops to about 31%, because the state material doesn't double when the general material does. In absolute terms it's still 44 questions of Nevada law, 14 more than the standalone Life exam carries.

## Nevada Property Insurance Exam Topic Breakdown

The Nevada Property exam runs 2 hours and contains 92 questions: 82 scored plus 10 unscored pretest items. The official outline distributes the 82 scored questions like this.

Types of Policies — 22 questions

Homeowners forms HO-2, HO-3, HO-4, HO-5, HO-6, and HO-8

Dwelling policies DP-1, DP-2, and DP-3

Commercial lines — commercial package policy, commercial property and causes-of-loss forms, business income, extra expense, equipment breakdown, businessowners policy, builders risk, cyber first-party coverage

Inland marine — personal articles floaters and commercial property floaters

National Flood Insurance Program

Earthquake, mobile homes, watercraft, farmowners, windstorm

Insurance Terms and Related Concepts — 15 questions

Insurable interest, risk, hazard, peril, and proximate cause

Loss valuation — actual cash value, replacement cost, market value, stated/agreed value, salvage value

Deductibles, indemnity, limits of liability, coinsurance and insurance to value

Cancellation, nonrenewal, vacancy and unoccupancy, blanket vs. specific

Policy Provisions and Contract Law — 13 questions

Declarations, insuring agreement, conditions, exclusions, definition of the insured

Duties of the insured, obligations of the insurer, mortgagee rights

Proof of loss, notice of claim, appraisal, other insurance, subrogation

Warranties, representations, and concealment; Fair Credit Reporting Act; Gramm-Leach-Bliley privacy; Terrorism Risk Insurance Act

Nevada Specific — 32 questions, or 39% of the scored exam

Nevada statutes and codes common to life, health, property, and casualty insurance (20 questions)

Nevada statutes common to property and casualty insurance (10 questions) — cancellation and nonrenewal, rate regulation, claims practices

Nevada statutes pertinent to property insurance only (2 questions) — inland marine and transportation

Note the shape of that state outline: only 2 of the 32 Nevada questions are property-specific. The rest is general Nevada insurance law that applies across every major line, which is exactly the material a national study course won't cover.

## Nevada Casualty Insurance Exam Topic Breakdown

The Nevada Casualty exam runs 2 hours 15 minutes and contains 99 questions: 89 scored plus 10 unscored pretest items. The official outline distributes the 89 scored questions like this.

Types of Policies, Bonds, and Related Terms — 23 questions

Commercial general liability — premises and operations, products and completed operations, Coverages A, B, and C, supplementary payments, who is an insured, occurrence vs. claims-made

Personal and business auto — liability, medical payments, physical damage, uninsured and underinsured motorists, types of auto, garagekeepers, exclusions

Workers' compensation and employers liability (general concepts; Nevada's own workers' comp law is tested in the state outline)

Crime — employee dishonesty, theft, robbery, burglary, forgery and alteration

Surety and fidelity bonds

Professional liability — errors and omissions, medical malpractice, D&O, EPLI, cyber liability, liquor liability

Umbrella and excess liability, and the businessowners policy

Insurance Terms and Related Concepts — 15 questions

Risk, hazards, indemnity, insurable interest, negligence, liability, occurrence

Loss valuation methods

Binders, warranties, representations, concealment, certificates of insurance, deposit premium and audit

Damages — compensatory (general and special) and punitive

Policy Provisions — 12 questions

Declarations, insuring agreement, conditions, exclusions and limitations, definition of the insured

Duties of the insured after a loss, cancellation and nonrenewal provisions, supplementary payments

Proof of loss, notice of claim, other insurance, subrogation, consent-to-settle provisions, Terrorism Risk Insurance Act

Nevada Specific — 39 questions, or 43.8% of the scored exam

Nevada statutes and codes common to life, health, property, and casualty insurance (20 questions)

Nevada statutes common to property and casualty insurance (10 questions)

Nevada statutes pertinent to casualty insurance only (9 questions) — including Nevada's uninsured and underinsured motorist provisions and its auto insurance requirements

Casualty carries the largest Nevada-specific block of any single-line exam, 39 questions. Nevada auto law alone can decide whether you pass.

## Topic Areas That Tend to Need Extra Study Time

Nevada doesn't publish topic-level failure data, so treat this as a study heuristic rather than a statistic. These areas carry the most detail per question:

Annuities. The variety of annuity types (immediate, deferred, fixed, variable, indexed) plus tax treatment and suitability creates substantial content.

Medicare. Medicare's parts, eligibility, enrollment periods, and coordination with other coverage is detailed material.

Nevada-specific content. Generic study materials don't cover Nevada law adequately.

Replacement rules. Specific Nevada replacement rules and required procedures.

Health plan types. HMO vs. PPO vs. POS vs. EPO distinctions, particularly which require referrals and how cost-sharing differs.

Suitability standards. Nevada's Annuity Best Interest standard adds complexity for annuity sales.

Long-term care insurance. LTC product complexity plus Nevada training requirements.

## Topic Areas Where Candidates Typically Succeed

Some topics tend to be more accessible:

Insurance basics. Concepts like insurable interest, contract law basics, and risk management tend to make sense to most candidates.

Term life insurance. Term life is conceptually straightforward.

Auto insurance basics. Most candidates have personal experience with auto insurance.

Federal laws (HIPAA, ERISA basics). Reasonably well-defined frameworks.

## How to Use Topic Breakdowns Strategically

Identify high-weight sections. Focus study time in proportion to scored question counts. On the Life, Health, Property, and Casualty exams, the Nevada Specific outline is the single largest section on the test — larger than any general-knowledge section.

Address weak topics. Topics where you struggle deserve extra study attention regardless of weight.

Don't ignore low-weight sections entirely. The smallest section on the Life exam is still 8 of the 80 scored questions — a full tenth of what you're graded on.

Practice across topic areas. Don't just study one area until you master it. Spread practice across all topics for better integration.

Take topic-specific practice quizzes. Most quality prep providers offer topic-specific practice. Use these to identify and address weaknesses.

## Every Nevada Producer Exam at a Glance

Scored questions, total questions including unscored pretest items, time allotted, and the size of the Nevada Specific outline — all from the Pearson VUE content outlines effective October 1, 2024:

Life. 80 scored (90 total), 2 hours. Nevada Specific: 30 questions, 37.5%.

Accident & Health. 88 scored (98 total), 2 hours 15 minutes. Nevada Specific: 38 questions, 43.2%.

Life and Health Combined. 144 scored (159 total), 3 hours 35 minutes. Nevada Specific: 44 questions, 30.6%.

Property. 82 scored (92 total), 2 hours. Nevada Specific: 32 questions, 39%.

Casualty. 89 scored (99 total), 2 hours 15 minutes. Nevada Specific: 39 questions, 43.8%.

Property and Casualty Combined. 141 scored (156 total), 3 hours 35 minutes. Nevada Specific: 41 questions, 29.1%.

Personal Lines. 100 scored (107 total), 2 hours 15 minutes. Nevada Specific: 25 questions, 25%.

Personal Lines is the outlier. It's the only Nevada producer exam where state-specific content sits at a quarter of the test. Every other exam runs higher, and the single-line Health and Casualty exams sit just under 44%.

Scores are reported on a scaled 0-to-100 basis with a cut of 70 set under NAC 683A.270 — the number on your report is not the percentage of questions you got right, and Pearson VUE does not publish a per-section pass threshold. You need the whole exam, which is why the section counts are a study-allocation tool rather than a target list.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- How can I find Nevada's official exam content outline? Download the Nevada Insurance Content Outlines (publication #122903) from pearsonvue.com/nv/insurance. It's a separate document from the Candidate Handbook, and it's the one that lists scored question counts by section.
- What percentage of the Nevada exam is state-specific? It varies a lot by line, and it's larger than most candidates expect: 37.5% on Life (30 of 80 scored), 43.2% on Health (38 of 88), 39% on Property (32 of 82), 43.8% on Casualty (39 of 89), 30.6% on the Life and Health combined exam (44 of 144), 29.1% on the Property and Casualty combined exam (41 of 141), and 25% on Personal Lines (25 of 100).
- Which topics should get the most study time? Nevada doesn't publish per-topic failure data, so no one can rank topics by failure rate. What the outlines do show is that the Nevada Specific section is the largest single block on each of the Life, Health, Property, and Casualty exams, which makes state law the highest-leverage material on the test.
- Should I focus on one topic until I master it? No. Spread study across all topic areas. Mastery in some topics doesn't compensate for weakness in others.
- Are the Nevada exam questions multiple choice? Yes. All Nevada insurance license exams are computer-based and multiple choice.
