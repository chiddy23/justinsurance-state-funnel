## Identify your licensing requirements

Before signing up for courses, it is essential that you fully understand your state’s guidelines. Georgia typically mandates a specific number of continuing education hours to maintain an active insurance license, though the exact requirements can vary depending on your license type and experience. You may also be required to complete a certain number of ethics-focused credits.

Staying on top of these details can save you from headaches later. Sometimes, license holders only discover they are missing a credit or two when it is nearly time to renew. Avoid last-minute stress by reviewing all the requirements at least once a year, especially if you hold multiple lines of authority. Make sure to verify:

• The total number of CE hours required for your particular license.  
• How often you must renew and complete CE for each renewal cycle.  
• Whether there are special ethics course requirements or new policies introduced by state authorities.

By keeping track of these essentials, you will be able to plan your study schedule and avoid any surprises.

## Prepare for the final exam

Most online Georgia insurance CE courses require you to pass a final exam once you have completed the necessary lessons and practice exercises. The good news is that reputable platforms break down complicated insurance topics into manageable sections, making it easier for you to absorb the information.

If an exam feels intimidating, remember that the goal is simply to confirm you understand essential concepts. By taking a step-by-step approach, you build confidence and knowledge at the same time. Before the final test, you can typically:

• Review study guides or charts provided by your CE platform.  
• Revisit any modules or lessons you struggled with during your initial review.  
• Take practice exams to get comfortable with the format.

These strategies can significantly boost your chances of passing on your first try. If you need extra clarification on certain topics, try reaching out to your course provider’s support team.

## Stay on top of renewals

Once you have completed your continuing education for one renewal cycle, you will still want to keep an eye on any new regulations or changes to your license requirements. It can be easy to forget about CE once you are done for the moment, but setting reminders can help you prepare better for the next renewal.

It is wise to schedule periodic check-ins, whether that means a quick online search to keep up with Georgia insurance regulations or verifying that your contact information is up to date with the state licensing board. If you anticipate changes in your lines of authority or expansions to other insurance products, check ahead to see if these adjustments require additional CE hours.
