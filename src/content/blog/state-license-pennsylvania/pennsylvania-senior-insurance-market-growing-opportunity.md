## Medicare Market Specifics

Medicare practice involves specific considerations:

Initial Enrollment Period (IEP). When seniors first qualify for Medicare (typically around 65 birthday).

Annual Enrollment Period (AEP). October 15 - December 7 each year. Substantial activity period for Medicare Advantage and Part D enrollment changes.

Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period. January 1 - March 31. Allows Medicare Advantage enrollees to change plans or return to Original Medicare.

Special Enrollment Periods (SEP). Various circumstances allowing enrollment outside standard periods (loss of employer coverage, moving, etc.).

Medicare Supplement Open Enrollment. Special protections for first-time Medicare enrollees in 6-month window.

Pennsylvania-specific considerations. Pennsylvania has substantial Medicare Advantage market activity with various plans available across the state.

## Long-Term Care Specialty Within Senior Practice

Pennsylvania has specific LTC training and policy requirements:

Pennsylvania LTC training framework:

Initial 8-hour state-approved LTC Training course (one-time)

4-hour ongoing LTC Training every 24 months

For LTC Partnership: initial 8 hours must include 1 hour on Medicaid Assistance Program

Pennsylvania LTC Partnership. Connects LTC insurance to Medicaid asset protection — particularly relevant for middle-class seniors concerned about asset protection.

Pennsylvania-specific LTC suitability. Specific suitability standards for Pennsylvania LTC sales.

LTC specialty integrates closely with senior insurance practice.

## Annuity Specialty for Seniors

Pennsylvania producers serving senior clients with annuities must:

Complete Annuity Best Interest training (4-hour one-time, per Section 403.1-B of Act 48). All producers with life and fixed annuities or variable life/variable line authority must complete.

Apply Pennsylvania suitability standards. Pennsylvania adopted enhanced standards aligned with NAIC 2020 Annuity Model Law.

Apply enhanced suitability for senior clients. Senior clients receive enhanced suitability attention in Pennsylvania.

Document recommendations carefully. Comprehensive analysis of suitability supporting recommendations.

Recognize diminished capacity. Senior clients with potential capacity issues require enhanced procedures.

Coordinate with family. Often appropriate to involve family members in senior client decisions (with client consent).
