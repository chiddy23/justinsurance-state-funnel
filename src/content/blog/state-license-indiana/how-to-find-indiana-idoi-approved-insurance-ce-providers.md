Indiana's CE system uses Sircon and NIPR for tracking CE completions and renewal submissions. IDOI-approved CE provider lists and approved courses are accessible through Sircon at sircon.com/Indiana. CE providers must be approved by the IDOI's Insurance Producer Education and [Continuing Education](/indiana/continuing-education) Commission. Completions are reported to Sircon or NIPR; certificates must be retained for audit purposes. Here's how to find and work with IDOI-approved Indiana CE providers efficiently.

## Where to Find Approved Providers

Primary official source: in.gov/idoi → Licensing → Continuing Education

Sircon: sircon.com/Indiana → "Look up education courses/credits" → "Approved Courses Inquiry" Same portal used for PLE course lookup and renewal submissions.

IDOI Licensing Division: Contact AgentLicensing@idoi.in.gov or (800) 622-4461 for CE provider and specialty training questions.

Multiple national providers and online platforms offer IDOI-approved Indiana CE — verify current Indiana approval before enrolling.

## Key Information to Verify

IDOI approval status for the specific course.

Credit type: Ethics, Life, Health, Property, Casualty, Annuity, LTC, NFIP Flood, etc.

Hours. Confirm exact hours per course.

Reporting. CE providers report to Sircon or NIPR. Verify transcript before renewal.

Professional organization participation: Up to 2 hours/cycle — requires documentation from organization. Contact IDOI for current process.

## The Renewal Submission Process

Complete 24 CE hours before renewal deadline (include specialty training if applicable)

Verify all completions in Sircon or NIPR CE transcript

Retain certificates for potential audit

Submit renewal through Sircon or NIPR

Pay $40 renewal fee before last day of birth month

Submit on time. Miss the deadline and reinstatement runs $160 — a flat $120 IDOI reinstatement fee plus the $40 renewal — and that option closes one year past expiration

## Specialty Training Through Approved Providers

Annuity Best Interest (4-hour one-time; 760 IAC 1-72-4.5): Search Sircon or contact AgentLicensing@idoi.in.gov for IDOI-approved Annuity Best Interest courses. Verify course specifically covers the NAIC Best Interest standard as adopted by Indiana.

LTC Training: Find IDOI-approved LTC courses through Sircon. Verify current Indiana-specific content requirements at in.gov/idoi.

NFIP Flood Training: Find IDOI-approved NFIP flood courses through Sircon or national CE providers with Indiana approval.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- How do I find IDOI-approved Indiana CE providers? Through sircon.com/Indiana → "Approved Courses Inquiry" and at in.gov/idoi → Licensing → CE. Contact the IDOI Licensing Division at AgentLicensing@idoi.in.gov or (800) 622-4461 for specific CE and specialty training questions.
- Do Indiana CE completions automatically appear in my Sircon transcript? CE providers are responsible for reporting completions to Sircon or NIPR. Most IDOI-approved providers report electronically — completions should appear within a few business days. Verify your transcript before submitting renewal. If a completion doesn't appear, contact your CE provider to confirm they reported it.
- Can I complete all 24 CE hours in one day in Indiana? Verify current IDOI daily CE limit policy at in.gov/idoi. Some states (like Minnesota) have an 8-hour daily maximum; Maryland has a 12-hour limit. Indiana may not have an explicit daily limit — confirm with IDOI before attempting to complete all CE in a single day.
- How do I claim the professional organization CE credit? Contact IDOI at AgentLicensing@idoi.in.gov for current procedures. Generally: provide a signed, dated letter from the professional organization's designee confirming your participation, specific committee or board role, and active status. The Commissioner applies the credit (up to 2 hours). Start this process early in the CE cycle — don't wait until the renewal deadline.
- Can I use CE completed for a professional designation toward Indiana CE requirements? Verify with IDOI. Many designation CE programs (CPCU, CLU, CIC, etc.) may offer IDOI-approved CE courses — but approval must be confirmed for Indiana specifically. The professional organization CE credit (IC 27-1-15.7-2.4) is a separate provision from designation CE and covers active organizational participation, not designation exam/course completion.
