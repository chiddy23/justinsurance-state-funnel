Renewing your Georgia insurance license is a straightforward online process — if you know the steps and you've met your [continuing education](/georgia/continuing-education) requirements. Miss the deadline or skip your CE, and renewal becomes more complicated. Understanding the process ahead of time keeps you compliant and active.

Here's exactly how to renew your Georgia insurance license online.

## Georgia's Renewal Cycle

Georgia insurance licenses renew on a 2-year cycle, and your expiration date is set by your birth month — not by when your license was originally issued. Under [O.C.G.A. § 33-23-18](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-33-insurance/ga-code-sect-33-23-18/), resident agent, subagent, adjuster, and counselor licenses are issued on a biennial basis and expire on the last day of the licensee's birth month. OCI also lists your exact renewal date in your online profile, so confirm it there before you plan around it.

Renewal applies to all Georgia insurance license types:

- Life, Accident, and Sickness

- Property and Casualty

- Personal Lines

- Limited lines

- Adjuster licenses

Each has its own CE requirements that must be satisfied before renewal.

## Before You Renew: Complete Your CE

Before you can renew, you must have completed your continuing education requirements for the cycle. For most Georgia insurance producers, this means:

- [24 hours of CE every 2-year cycle](https://oci.georgia.gov/agents-agency-licensing/agent-licensing-renewals/continuing-education)

- [3 hours must be in Ethics](https://oci.georgia.gov/agents-agency-licensing/agent-licensing-renewals/continuing-education) (or specific ethics-focused content)

- The remaining 21 hours can be in approved elective insurance topics

Verify your CE completion in OCI's system before attempting to renew. If your CE isn't complete and reported, renewal will be rejected.

## Step 1: Check Your CE Status

Log into OCI's online producer portal or use Sircon/NIPR to confirm:

- Your current CE hour total for the active cycle

- Whether your 3-hour ethics requirement is satisfied

- Whether any specialty CE (LTC, annuity training) is current if applicable

- Whether your CE is fully reported by your providers

If hours are missing or not yet reported, contact your CE provider first. Most reporting issues are resolved at the provider level.

## Step 2: Access the Renewal Portal

Georgia insurance license renewal is typically completed through:

- NIPR (nipr.com) — the National Insurance Producer Registry

- Sircon (sircon.com)

- OCI's online portal — accessible through oci.georgia.gov

All three serve as authorized portals. Use whichever you're comfortable with — the outcome is the same.

## Step 3: Log In and Start Your Renewal

You'll need:

- Your Georgia license number

- Your National Producer Number (NPN)

- Your personal identifying information (date of birth, Social Security number)

Once logged in, select the renewal option for your license.

## Step 4: Complete the Renewal Application

The online renewal application will ask you to:

- Confirm your current personal and business information

- Update your address or contact information if needed

- Answer disclosure questions about any new criminal history, regulatory actions, bankruptcies, or other reportable events since your last renewal

- Confirm CE compliance

Answer disclosure questions honestly. Any new events since your last renewal must be disclosed. OCI cross-references your answers against available records.

## Step 5: Pay the Renewal Fee

Georgia's license renewal fee is paid online through the portal. Fees vary by license type but are generally $100 or less for most individual producer licenses.

Payment is typically by credit card. You'll receive a confirmation email and receipt after submission.

## Step 6: Receive Confirmation

After OCI processes your renewal:

- You'll receive email confirmation

- Your license status updates to "active" for the new 2-year cycle

- Your renewal date resets based on the new cycle

For most straightforward renewals, processing is quick — often same-day or within a few business days.

## When to Renew

Don't wait until the deadline. Renew at least 30 days before your expiration date to give yourself buffer for any issues that arise (CE reporting gaps, disclosure questions, processing delays).

Early renewal is fine. Georgia generally allows renewal starting several weeks before the deadline. Early renewal doesn't shorten your new cycle — your next expiration is still the last day of your birth month two years out.

## What Happens If You Miss the Renewal Deadline

If you don't renew on time:

- Your license enters lapsed status

- You cannot conduct insurance business until it's reinstated

- You'll face late fees and potentially reinstatement fees

- You may need to complete missing CE plus additional reinstatement requirements

Grace period. Georgia typically allows a short grace period during which you can renew with late fees without full reinstatement. After the grace period, full reinstatement procedures apply.

Extended non-compliance. If you let the license lapse for an extended period (typically over a year), you may be required to reapply from scratch — including retaking [prelicense](/georgia/prelicensing) education and the state exam.

## Common Renewal Problems

Missing CE reporting. Your CE provider didn't submit completion to OCI on time. Contact the provider to retransmit.

Missed ethics requirement. You completed 24 hours but didn't complete the required 3 hours of ethics content. Complete an ethics course and have it reported.

Incomplete specialty training. LTC or annuity training wasn't completed or reported. Complete required training and have it reported before renewing.

Disclosure issues. New reportable events since last renewal need proper documentation. Gather supporting documents and disclose completely.

Address or contact mismatches. Incorrect information in OCI's system can cause delays. Update your profile before renewing.

## Keeping Your Next Renewal Smooth

Track your renewal date. Add it to your calendar, phone, and any business planning system you use.

Complete CE throughout the cycle. Don't wait until month 23 of your 24-month cycle to start. Spread CE completion across the cycle.

Check OCI status quarterly. Log in and verify your CE is being reported correctly. Catch problems early.

Use reputable CE providers. Approved providers report reliably. Non-approved or disreputable providers create reporting nightmares.

Keep documentation. Save every CE completion certificate indefinitely.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

1. How often do I have to renew my Georgia insurance license? Every 2 years, and your license expires on the last day of your birth month ([O.C.G.A. § 33-23-18](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-33-insurance/ga-code-sect-33-23-18/)). Your deadline follows your birth month, not the date your license was originally issued — check OCI's portal to confirm your exact date.

2. Can I renew my license online? Yes. Renewal is completed online through NIPR, Sircon, or OCI's portal. Paper renewals are uncommon.

3. What happens if I don't complete my CE before renewal? Renewal will be rejected. Complete your CE, have it reported to OCI, then attempt renewal. This may cost you time and possibly late fees.

4. Do I have to pay a renewal fee even if I didn't sell any insurance during the cycle? Yes. Renewal fees apply regardless of production. The fee maintains your license as active.

5. Can someone else renew my license for me? You must complete the disclosure questions yourself honestly. Administrative staff can help navigate the portal, but you must verify and submit the application.
