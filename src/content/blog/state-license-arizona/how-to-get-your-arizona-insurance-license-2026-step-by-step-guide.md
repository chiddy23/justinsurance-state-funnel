Arizona’s licensing path looks lighter on paper than Illinois or Ohio because the Arizona Department of Insurance and Financial Institutions (DIFI) emphasizes passing the licensing exam and maintaining a real business footprint before you sell. The industry association IIABAZ summarizes the baseline resident checklist—**18** years old, maintain a principal place of business, pass the licensing exam, submit fingerprints when required, and pay the proper fees—on its [Licensing & Continuing Education](https://www.iiabaz.com/licensing-ce/) page. NIPR’s [Arizona overview](https://nipr.com/licensing-center/state-information/arizona) adds the vendor detail you cannot skip: PSI schedules Arizona insurance exams, and exam scores remain valid for **12** months. If you treat Arizona as “exam-only,” you will still fail compliance if you ignore continuing education, long-term care training, or the principal-place rule.

## Pass PSI, but first prove you can legally sit for the line

IIABAZ notes limited exceptions—nonresidents in good standing, certain new residents with clearance letters within **90** days of canceling a prior home-state license, and narrow limited lines such as travel insurance—where the exam is not required. Everyone else should assume PSI registration is mandatory. PSI’s national test-taker portal is linked from NIPR’s Arizona page; keep the PSI phone **800-733-9267** from that same overview handy when web scheduling throws errors.

## Budget continuing education before you celebrate the new license

IIABAZ states most Arizona resident producers must complete **48** hours of Arizona-approved CE each licensing term, including at least **6** hours of ethics, unless they meet every prong of a narrow grandfathering test tied to continuous licensure since **January 1, 1995**, no nonresident history, and no disciplinary orders. That **48**/**6** split matters for agency compliance plans more than any single exam pass.

## Long-term care authority is statutory, not optional marketing training

Arizona Revised Statutes § **20-1691.12** (published on the Arizona State Legislature site) requires **8** hours of initial Arizona-approved long-term care training before you sell, solicit, or negotiate LTC, then **4** hours during each subsequent **two**-year period after the period in which you finished the initial **8** hours. IIABAZ repeats those numbers for producers who prefer plain English. Nonresidents can satisfy Arizona with substantially similar home-state training, but you still need documentation DIFI can audit.

## Flood training layers on top of CE if you touch NFIP business

IIABAZ points producers who sell federal flood policies to **3** hours of Arizona-approved flood education that also counts toward the **48**-hour CE bucket. That is separate from PSI exam prep; it is a post-license obligation tied to NFIP rules.

## Use DIFI’s producer application hub when you are ready to file

IIABAZ links directly to DIFI’s [individual producer application](https://difi.az.gov/producers/producer-agentbroker/producer-agentbroker-license-application-individual) workflow. NIPR’s Arizona page still recommends LicenseHub for many transactions, but residents should read DIFI’s instructions each time—Arizona frequently updates PDFs and background-question language faster than aggregators mirror them.

## Compare Arizona’s post-license load with Georgia and Pennsylvania

Georgia’s OCI page stresses Pearson VUE exam fees and now lists **8.0**-hour prelicensing per major line under Rule **120-2-3-.08** on the Georgia [education providers page](https://oci.georgia.gov/agents-agency-licensing/education-providers-courses-and-instructors). Pennsylvania’s [initial licensing page](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/insurance/licensing/licensees/initial-insurance-producer-licensing-process.html) documents **$55** resident application fees and PSI scheduling with IdentoGO code **1KGBGJ**. Arizona’s different: IIABAZ highlights exam passage plus **48**/**6** CE, while LTC sits in **A.R.S. § 20-1691.12**. If you are building a tri-state desert recruiting plan, do not copy Pennsylvania’s fingerprint timing into Arizona—rules diverge.

## Principal place of business means real records, not a mail drop

IIABAZ quotes the general requirement: maintain a location where you keep usual and customary transaction records. Regulators interpret that as a workspace you control, not a shared mailbox in Sedona. Document leases, utility bills, or agency agreements before you answer background questions on the DIFI application.

## Bottom line

Confirm you need PSI (not an exemption), pass within the **12**-month NIPR score window, file through DIFI or NIPR as instructed, and immediately plan **48**/**6** CE plus **8**/**4** LTC training if you touch those markets. Drill with [Arizona practice exams](https://justinsuranceco.com/arizona/practice-exam), read the [insurance exam guide](https://justinsuranceco.com/insurance-exam-guide) for vendor-specific traps, and book [Arizona prelicensing](https://justinsuranceco.com/arizona/prelicensing) if you still want structured hours even when Arizona emphasizes examination performance.
