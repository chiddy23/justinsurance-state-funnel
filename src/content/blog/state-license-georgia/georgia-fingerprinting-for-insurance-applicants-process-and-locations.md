If you're pursuing a Georgia insurance license, fingerprinting is a mandatory step you can't skip. It's required for the criminal background check OCI conducts on every applicant. Fortunately, the process is straightforward when you know what to expect.

Here's exactly how Georgia fingerprinting works for insurance applicants — where to go, what to bring, and what happens afterward.

## Why Georgia Requires Fingerprinting

Georgia's Office of Commissioner of Insurance requires fingerprinting as part of the background check on all insurance license applicants. Your fingerprints are submitted through both the Georgia Bureau of Investigation (GBI) and the FBI to check for criminal history at the state and federal levels.

This isn't unique to Georgia — most states require it. OCI uses the results to ensure that people entering the insurance industry meet the character and fitness standards expected of licensed professionals.

## Where to Get Fingerprinted in Georgia

Georgia uses approved electronic fingerprinting providers. The primary provider is IdentoGO (operated by IDEMIA), which has locations throughout the state.

To schedule your appointment:

- Go to uenroll.identogo.com

- Enter the Georgia service code for insurance applicants (OCI can provide this)

- Select a location near you

- Choose your appointment time

IdentoGO has locations throughout Georgia:

- Atlanta metro area — multiple locations in Atlanta, Sandy Springs, Alpharetta, Marietta, Decatur, and surrounding areas

- Savannah area — locations for coastal Georgia applicants

- Augusta area — serving Augusta, Martinez, and East Georgia

- Columbus area — serving Columbus, LaGrange, and West Georgia

- Macon area — serving Macon and Middle Georgia

- Athens area — serving Athens, Watkinsville, and Northeast Georgia

- Rome, Gainesville, Valdosta, Albany — and many other Georgia cities

Appointments are typically available within 1–5 business days, often faster. Check availability at multiple nearby locations if your preferred site is booked.

## What to Bring to Your Appointment

1. Your IdentoGO registration confirmation. Bring a printed or digital copy of your appointment confirmation.

2. Valid government-issued photo ID. Options include:

- Driver's license (Georgia or out-of-state)

- Passport

- Military ID

- Permanent resident card

The name on your ID must match the name on your appointment registration.

3. Payment method. The fingerprinting fee is typically $50–$75, though fees can vary. If you haven't paid online during registration, you'll pay at the appointment. Accepted payment methods vary by location but usually include credit cards.

4. Your Social Security number. You'll need this during the fingerprinting process.

## What Happens During the Appointment

The appointment itself is quick — typically 10 to 15 minutes from start to finish:

Step 1 — Check-in. You'll present your ID and confirmation.

Step 2 — Fingerprint capture. Fingerprints are captured digitally — no ink involved. The technician will roll each finger on a glass scanner plate. The process is painless and takes just a few minutes.

Step 3 — Electronic submission. Your fingerprints are submitted electronically to GBI and FBI databases for processing.

Step 4 — Confirmation. You'll receive confirmation that your fingerprints were captured and submitted. Keep this documentation.

## When to Schedule Fingerprinting

You don't have to wait until after you pass your exam. Many applicants schedule fingerprinting while they're still studying or shortly after passing, so results are in OCI's system by the time they submit their license application.

This is the smartest approach — it removes one of the most common causes of application delays.

## How Long Until OCI Gets Your Results

Electronic fingerprint results are typically submitted to OCI within 24 to 72 hours of your appointment. Processing can occasionally run longer during high-volume periods.

You can check your application status in OCI's systems or through NIPR/Sircon to confirm when results are received.

## What If Something Comes Up in Your Background Check?

If your background check reveals issues, OCI will review them as part of your application. Not every record results in denial — OCI evaluates the nature of the issue, how long ago it occurred, and other factors.

If you have concerns about your background before investing in the licensing process, consider:

- Requesting informal guidance from OCI before applying

- Consulting with an attorney experienced in Georgia administrative matters

- Preparing documentation (court records, rehabilitation evidence) to submit with your application

## What If Your Prints Are Rejected?

Occasionally, fingerprints are rejected due to capture quality. If this happens:

- IdentoGO will notify you

- You'll typically schedule a re-capture appointment (no additional charge)

- The process repeats

- OCI won't complete your application until acceptable prints are on file

## Fingerprinting for Non-Resident Applicants

If you're applying for a non-resident Georgia license (you're licensed in another state and want to conduct business with Georgia clients), fingerprinting requirements may differ. Non-resident applicants typically rely on fingerprints already on file in their home state, though Georgia may require additional submission in some cases. Check OCI's non-resident application guidance for specifics.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I use a different fingerprinting service besides IdentoGO? Georgia uses OCI-approved providers. IdentoGO is the primary provider, but other OCI-approved services may exist. Using a non-approved provider means your prints won't be accepted.

2. How long does it take for OCI to receive my fingerprint results? Typically 24 to 72 hours. Processing can take longer during high-volume periods or if there are issues requiring additional review.

3. Do I need to get fingerprinted every time I renew my license? No. Fingerprinting is typically a one-time requirement for initial licensure in Georgia. Renewal doesn't require re-fingerprinting.

4. What if I've already been fingerprinted for another Georgia state agency? OCI typically requires its own fingerprint submission through its approved process specifically for insurance licensing. Prints submitted for another agency don't automatically transfer.

5. How much does Georgia insurance fingerprinting cost? Fingerprinting fees are typically $50–$75, varying by provider and location. Confirm current fees when scheduling your appointment.
