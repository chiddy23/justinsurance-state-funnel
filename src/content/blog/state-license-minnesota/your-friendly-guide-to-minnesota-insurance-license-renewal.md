## Grasp the basics of renewal

If you're looking to keep your Minnesota insurance career on track, renewing your license is an essential part of the process. In Minnesota, you typically need to renew your producer license every two years to stay compliant and able to sell insurance products. Thankfully, the path to renewal is straightforward once you understand the basic requirements.

Start by confirming which lines of authority you hold, such as life and health or property and casualty. Each line may have slightly different [continuing education](/minnesota/continuing-education) rules, so knowing your license type helps you plan your renewal effectively. From there, you can move on to the next key step: completing your required training hours.

## Complete your continuing education

Most Minnesota insurance professionals must fulfill [Minnesota continuing education](/minnesota/continuing-education) (CE) credits to renew. The exact number varies depending on your license type, but the standard requirement is 24 credit hours every two years. These hours keep you current with laws, regulations, and industry changes that affect your everyday work.

The ethics requirement is a topic many new producers overlook. You must complete at least 3 hours on ethics-related content in every renewal cycle. It's also important to stick with approved CE providers recognized by the [Minnesota Department of Commerce](/minnesota/requirements). If you’re not certain about a course’s approval status, contact the provider or check the state’s online resources for verification.

## Meet important renewal deadlines

You’ll want to keep a close eye on deadlines to avoid license lapses. Minnesota typically aligns your license expiration date with your birthday or the anniversary of your initial licensure, so it’s wise to start the renewal process well before that date arrives. If you wait until the last minute, you risk running out of time to complete your CE courses or submit your paperwork.

Plan to finish your courses at least 30 days before your license expires. That buffer gives you enough time to handle any technical issues, retake a course exam if necessary, and ensure all CE hours are properly recorded. Once you’ve met your CE requirements, you can submit your renewal application and the corresponding fee through the appropriate state portal.

## Avoid common renewal pitfalls

One of the biggest hurdles is waiting too long to fulfill your CE hours. Procrastination leads to stress and, in some cases, a lapse in your license if you miss the deadline completely. Another pitfall is neglecting the ethics requirement. Even if you rack up your total hours, leaving out ethics training will delay your renewal until you address the shortfall.

It's also common to accidentally choose courses that don't match your lines of authority. Double-check that your chosen classes align with life, health, property, or casualty content, depending on your specific license. Lastly, keep your contact information updated with the Minnesota Department of Commerce. Missing a renewal notice or other important communication can complicate the process and result in extra paperwork or fees.

## Frequently asked questions

1.  How do I check my CE credits on file?  
    You can view your CE record on the Minnesota Department of Commerce website by logging into your licensee account. Make sure to confirm everything there matches the courses you’ve taken.
2.  What if I miss the renewal deadline?  
    If your license lapses, you may have to halt all selling activities or even repeat [Minnesota prelicensing](/minnesota/prelicensing) before it’s reinstated. You’ll generally need to complete your CE hours and pay any reinstatement fees before returning to work.
3.  How many times can I renew my license?  
    There’s no limit on the number of renewals. As long as you keep up with CE credits and submit your application on time, you can renew indefinitely.
4.  Is the renewal process different for each line of authority?  
    The overall steps are similar, but some lines may have additional course topics or requirements. Always verify that your classes match your license type.
5.  How do I quickly find an approved CE provider?  
    Many organizations offer Minnesota-approved courses. One option is JustInsurance, where you can find a range of classes that fulfill state criteria. Always confirm that any course is state-approved before enrolling.
