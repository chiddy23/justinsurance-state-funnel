Passing your exam is a big deal. But passing the exam doesn't automatically mean your license gets approved. The Texas Department of Insurance reviews every application — and knowing what they look at can save you from unnecessary delays or surprises.

Here's what TDI actually checks when you submit your application.

## Your Exam Results

The first thing TDI verifies is that you passed the state licensing exam through Pearson VUE. Your results are submitted electronically, so there's nothing extra you need to do — but your application won't move forward until that pass is confirmed in the system.

## Your Fingerprint and Background Check

Texas requires a criminal background check for all insurance license applicants, processed through IdentoGO. TDI checks both state and federal criminal history databases.

Having a criminal record doesn't automatically disqualify you — but certain convictions, especially those involving fraud, dishonesty, or financial crimes, can result in denial. If you have a prior conviction, you can request a pre-application determination from TDI before investing time and money into the process. TDI will give you a non-binding assessment of how your history might affect your application.

## Your Application Disclosures

The application asks a series of yes/no questions about your background — prior license denials, criminal history, regulatory actions, bankruptcies, and more. Answer these honestly. TDI cross-references your answers against background check results. Misrepresentation on a license application is taken seriously and can result in denial or future disciplinary action.

## Prelicense Education — Not Required in Texas

Texas doesn't require prelicensing education for standard producer licenses, so there's nothing for TDI to verify here. If you choose to take an [optional prelicense course](/texas/prelicensing) to prepare for the exam, TDI won't ask for a completion certificate — the course simply helps you get ready for the Pearson VUE exam itself.

## Your Application Fee

The non-refundable application fee is $50, paid through Sircon or NIPR when you submit. Your application won't be processed without it.

## How Long Does TDI Take to Review Your Application?

Most applications are processed within 5 to 10 business days if everything is in order. Delays happen when fingerprint results are pending, disclosures require additional review, or there are inconsistencies in your application. Submitting complete and accurate information upfront is the fastest way through.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- Can I apply before I complete fingerprinting? Yes, but your license won't be issued until fingerprint results are cleared. Many applicants submit their application and fingerprints simultaneously to save time.
- What if TDI denies my application? You have the right to request a hearing and appeal the decision. TDI will provide a written explanation of the denial reason.
- Do I need to disclose traffic violations? Minor traffic violations typically don't need to be disclosed. The application specifies what types of criminal history must be reported — read each question carefully.
- Can someone with a past bankruptcy get a Texas insurance license? Bankruptcy alone doesn't disqualify you. TDI evaluates the full picture of your background, not just one factor.
- What's the difference between Sircon and NIPR? Both are TDI-approved portals for submitting license applications. Either works — use whichever you're more comfortable with. The fee and outcome are the same.
