The single most preventable cause of last-minute NJ insurance license renewal problems is not missing CE hours — it is not knowing that hours are missing until it is too late to fix them. New Jersey's CE transcript is available online 24 hours a day, free, with no account required. And yet producers regularly arrive at their renewal deadline to discover that a course they completed weeks earlier never made it onto their official transcript — because they never checked. Tracking your NJ CE credits throughout your renewal period is not a bureaucratic chore. It is the single most reliable safeguard against a compliance failure that could cost you your license.

## Where NJ CE Transcripts Actually Live

This is the first place producers go wrong, so it is worth being precise: New Jersey's official CE transcript is **not** kept on Sircon or NIPR. The Department of Banking and Insurance contracted **PSI Services LLC** to administer the state's continuing education credit banking system, and PSI's CE site is the record DOBI itself points licensees to.

Your NJ CE transcript lives at **[ce.psiexams.com](https://ce.psiexams.com/)**. Vertafore's own Sircon State Information Center for New Jersey lists the same thing: New Jersey's CE Administrator is PSI Services, LLC.

Here is what each platform is actually for:

**PSI (ce.psiexams.com)** — the official CE credit bank. Your transcript, the approved course and provider search, and the classroom course offering calendar all live here. This is the system your CE provider reports your completions into.

**NIPR (nipr.com)** — the renewal transaction. DOBI directs resident producers to NIPR's Electronic Resident Licensing site to submit the renewal itself. NIPR is not a CE transcript source.

**Sircon (sircon.com)** — Vertafore's licensing platform. Producers and agencies use it for licensing and renewal transactions and for looking up approved education providers. It is not the state's CE database of record.

**DOBI licensee search** — confirms your license number, license status, and expiration date. It is not a CE compliance lookup; DOBI does not publish one.

One important limitation, straight from PSI's New Jersey producer FAQ: non-resident New Jersey licensees are not required to meet New Jersey CE requirements and cannot view a transcript. If you hold a non-resident NJ license, your CE obligation runs to your home state.

## How to Check Your NJ CE Transcript

Step 1: Go to [ce.psiexams.com](https://ce.psiexams.com/) and click the **Continuing Education Transcript** link. There is no login, no account, and no fee.

Step 2: Enter your last name and your New Jersey license number. Drop any leading zeros in the license number — this is the single most common reason a lookup returns nothing. If you do not know your license number, look it up on DOBI's licensee search or read it off your license.

Step 3: Review the transcript. It displays your CE review period begin date, your CE review period end date, and every course credited to you during that review period.

Step 4: Verify that every course you have completed appears, that ethics courses are carrying ethics credit, and that your running total is tracking toward 24.

Step 5: Confirm your renewal date. New Jersey producer licenses run on a two-year term, and DOBI sends renewal notices at least 30 days in advance — but do not wait for the notice. Check your expiration date on DOBI's licensee search and work backward from it.

## What to Look For When Reviewing Your Transcript

**Total hours completed:** Your running total should be progressing toward 24 over the course of the two-year term. If you are 18 months into your renewal cycle and have only 8 hours on transcript, you have a problem that needs to be addressed now — not in the final month.

**Ethics hours:** Confirm that at least 3 of your completed credits carry an ethics designation. New Jersey requires 3 ethics credits per licensing term, one of which may be satisfied with an insurance fraud credit. General CE hours do not satisfy the ethics requirement even if the course content touched on ethical topics — the credit type on the reported completion is what controls.

**No classroom minimum:** New Jersey does not impose a minimum number of classroom or "classroom equivalent" hours. N.J.A.C. 11:17-3.6 requires 24 credits including 3 ethics credits, and PSI's approved course search lists both classroom and self-study courses as creditable. If a competitor's marketing has you counting live hours toward a 12-hour quota, that quota does not exist. Your transcript may still label courses by delivery method — that is a description of how you took the course, not a separate requirement to satisfy.

**Duplicate courses:** Per DOBI policy, a CE course may only be taken once during a compliance period. Duplicate credits will not be applied toward your final compliance total, so scan your transcript for repeats before you buy anything else.

**Specialty training:** If you sell annuities, verify that your annuity training appears. If you sell LTC products, verify your training is on file. If you write flood coverage, verify your NFIP training is recorded.

**Reported vs. completed:** A course you finished last week may not appear yet if the provider has not submitted the roster. PSI advises allowing 24 hours for submitted credits to process. If a recently completed course is missing, contact the provider first — not the state.

## Common Transcript Issues and How to Resolve Them

**Missing course:** A course you completed does not appear on your transcript. PSI's own guidance is to contact the course provider and ask them to post your credits, and to verify that the provider has your correct last name and license number on file. If the provider says the credits were already posted and they still do not appear, the provider needs to re-submit. Keep every CE certificate in your files — it is your evidence of completion if a reporting dispute arises.

**Nothing comes up at all:** Before assuming your record is empty, re-run the lookup with the leading zeros stripped from your license number, and confirm you entered your last name exactly as DOBI has it. A non-resident license will also return no transcript, by design.

**Wrong credit type:** A course appears on your transcript but is classified as general CE rather than ethics. This is a provider reporting issue — contact the provider and request a correction to the credit type on the reported completion. If the course was not DOBI-approved for ethics credit in the first place, the credit type cannot be changed and the course does not satisfy the ethics requirement regardless of its content.

**Duplicate course blocked:** You completed a course you already took in the same compliance period. Duplicate credits are not applied. Courses from a prior term can be repeated.

**Carryover not reflecting:** If you completed more than 24 credits in your previous term, up to 12 excess credits carry over to the next renewal term — once, and never ethics credits. If the carryover is not reflected, raise it with DOBI's Education unit.

**When to escalate to DOBI:** If the provider has re-submitted and the credit still does not appear, or a course's approval status is in question, that is a state issue. DOBI's Education unit handles continuing education and course approval at **insed@dobi.nj.gov**, 609-292-7272 or 609-940-7480. General producer licensing questions go to **inslic@dobi.nj.gov**, 609-292-7272. PSI's CE support line is 1-877-526-6833.

## Build in a 30-Day Buffer

Finish your CE at least 30 days before your license expiration date. That buffer buys you:

Time for your provider to submit the roster and for PSI to process it

Time to identify and correct a transcript discrepancy

Time to complete any missing hours if a gap turns up

Time to get the renewal itself through NIPR

Your hours technically only need to be banked before you submit your renewal application — but a producer who checks the transcript 30 days out and finds a 3-hour gap can fix it. A producer who checks the night before expiration usually cannot.

## Setting Up a CE Calendar

The most effective approach to CE compliance is treating it as a recurring calendar obligation, not a deadline-driven scramble. A simple CE tracking system:

Set a calendar reminder 18 months into each renewal term to pull your PSI transcript

Set a second reminder at 90 days before expiration

Set a final reminder at 30 days before expiration as your hard deadline for completing all hours

After each completed course, re-check your PSI transcript within a few business days to confirm it was reported

This three-reminder system catches problems early enough to fix them without stress.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where is my official New Jersey CE transcript, and how long does it take for a course to show up?**

Your official transcript is maintained by PSI Services LLC, DOBI's CE credit banking vendor, at [ce.psiexams.com](https://ce.psiexams.com/) — click "Continuing Education Transcript" and enter your last name and license number with leading zeros dropped. Reporting speed depends on your provider, not on the state: PSI advises allowing 24 hours for submitted credits to process, but the clock does not start until your provider actually uploads the roster, and some providers batch classroom rosters after the session. If a completed course has not appeared within a few business days, contact your CE provider with your certificate of completion and ask them to confirm the completion was submitted.

**Do I need a Sircon or NIPR account to check my NJ CE transcript?**

No. The PSI transcript lookup requires no account at all — just your last name and license number. Sircon and NIPR serve different purposes: NIPR is where DOBI directs resident producers to submit the renewal application itself, and Sircon is Vertafore's licensing platform for licensing and renewal transactions and education provider lookups. Neither is New Jersey's CE database of record. Use PSI for transcript monitoring throughout your term, and NIPR for the renewal submission.

**Can I check my CE compliance on the DOBI website?**

DOBI's licensee search will confirm your license number, status, and expiration date, which is exactly what you need before running a transcript lookup. It is not a CE compliance tool — DOBI does not publish a CE-hours lookup on its own site, and it directs licensees to PSI at ce.psiexams.com or 1-877-526-6833 to check credits.

**What should I do if a completed course is missing and my renewal deadline is approaching?**

Contact your CE provider immediately with your certificate of completion and ask them to re-submit the completion to PSI. Verify that the provider has your correct last name and license number — a name or license-number mismatch is the most common reason credits post but never attach to your record. If the provider has re-submitted and the credit still does not appear, escalate to DOBI's Education unit at insed@dobi.nj.gov (609-292-7272 or 609-940-7480) with your name, license number, NPN, the provider's name, the course, your completion date, and a copy of your certificate. Do not wait to see if it resolves itself.

**How do I verify that my ethics hours are correctly classified?**

Each entry on your PSI transcript carries a credit type. Ethics credits will be designated as such; general CE credits will not. The designation on the reported completion is controlling — if a course you intended to count toward the 3-hour ethics requirement is reported as general CE, it does not satisfy the ethics requirement regardless of its content. Contact your provider to confirm whether the course was DOBI-approved for ethics credit. If it was not, you will need an additional approved ethics course before renewal. Remember that ethics credits never carry over from a prior term, so you cannot close an ethics gap with banked hours.

Tracking your NJ CE credits throughout your renewal period — not just at the deadline — is the difference between a smooth renewal and an avoidable compliance emergency. Three checks per renewal term, thirty days of buffer before your deadline, and a certificate on file for every course covers every scenario.
