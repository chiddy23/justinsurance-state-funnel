Completing 24 hours of [continuing education](/illinois/continuing-education) doesn't matter if those hours aren't from an IDOI-approved provider. In Illinois, only courses offered by approved providers count toward your CE requirement — and knowing how to verify approval protects you from wasting time and money on courses that won't credit toward renewal.

Here's exactly how to find IDOI-approved CE providers in Illinois.

## Why IDOI Approval Matters

The Illinois Department of Insurance regulates who can offer CE to licensed agents and adjusters. To be considered an approved provider:

The provider must be registered with IDOI

Each individual course must be reviewed and approved

Course content must meet IDOI standards for accuracy and relevance

Approval is typically tied to a specific license type and topic area

If a provider or course isn't approved, your completion doesn't get reported to IDOI, and the hours don't count toward your CE requirement — even if the course content is excellent on its own.

## Where to Search for Approved Providers

IDOI's online producer portal. IDOI maintains records of approved CE providers and courses. Access through insurance.illinois.gov.

Sircon (sircon.com). Sircon is an authorized platform that provides access to approved CE provider and course information for Illinois. Many agents find Sircon's interface easier to navigate than IDOI's portal.

NIPR. The National Insurance Producer Registry (nipr.com) also provides some information about CE providers and courses.

Through these platforms you can:

Search for providers by name

Search for courses by topic or category

Verify approval status before enrolling

Check course approval numbers

## Warning Signs of Non-Approved Providers

Be cautious with providers that:

Don't list their IDOI course approval numbers

Can't confirm they'll report completion directly to IDOI

Offer "insurance CE" without specifying Illinois approval

Use vague language like "may count toward CE" rather than confirming approval

Are priced dramatically lower than other providers without a clear reason

Have no verifiable business presence or track record

Reputable providers are transparent about their approval status. They list IDOI approval numbers clearly and explain exactly what license types the course qualifies for.

## What to Verify Before Enrolling

Before paying for any CE course, confirm:

- The provider is IDOI-approved. Check their status through IDOI's portal or Sircon, or ask for their provider ID number.
- The specific course is approved. Provider approval doesn't automatically mean every course they offer is approved. Each course has its own approval.
- The course matches your license type. A course approved for P&C licensees won't count for Life and Health holders. Confirm the course applies to your specific license.
- The course is current. Make sure you're taking the current version of any course, especially ethics courses that may have been updated.
- Reporting is included. Approved providers report your completion to IDOI electronically — typically within days to weeks of completion. Ask the provider to confirm this before you enroll.

## Common Course Topics That Qualify

IDOI approves courses across many topics, including:

Life insurance products and provisions

Health insurance and Medicare

Annuities and suitability

Long-term care insurance and long-term care partnership

Illinois law updates

Ethics and agent conduct

Risk management

Property and casualty topics (for P&C licensees)

Adjuster-specific topics (for adjusters)

The topic has to be approved for your specific license — courses that don't align with your license authority won't count.

## How to Use IDOI's System Effectively

Check your CE records at multiple points in your renewal cycle:

At the halfway point of your 2-year cycle — confirm you're on track

At the 60-day mark before renewal — last chance to fix anything missing

At the 30-day mark before renewal — final verification

Log in to confirm:

Your current CE hours and status

Your completion history

Which hours have been reported by which providers

Your renewal deadline

Whether you've completed the Ethics requirement

Checking regularly catches reporting issues early, when they're easy to fix.

## What If a Course Doesn't Appear on Your Record?

If you've completed a course but don't see it in your IDOI record within 30 days:

Step 1 — Contact the course provider. Ask them to verify they reported your completion. Most issues are resolved at this level. Providers can retransmit or correct submissions if there's been an error.

Step 2 — Keep your completion certificate. Every approved course issues a certificate after completion. Save it — digitally and printed. It's your proof if there's ever a dispute.

Step 3 — Contact IDOI. If the provider reports correctly but IDOI still doesn't show your hours, contact IDOI directly with your certificate. They can investigate and correct their record when documentation supports it.

## Comparing Providers

When choosing between approved providers, consider:

Price. Reasonable but not unusually low. Very low prices can signal poor quality.

Format. Online self-paced, webinar, live, printed. Choose what fits your learning style — keeping in mind that the mandatory 3-hour ethics course must be live classroom or webinar, not self-paced.

Reporting reliability. How quickly and consistently they report to IDOI.

Course currency. Are courses updated regularly? Ethics courses especially benefit from being current.

Customer support. Can you reach someone if you have questions or issues?

Course selection. Do they offer the specific topics you need, including ethics and any specialty training?

Track record. How long have they been operating? What do other agents say about them?

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

- Do I submit CE completions to IDOI myself? No. In Illinois, the approved course provider submits your completion electronically. You don't self-report.
- How quickly should hours appear in IDOI's records after I complete a course? Usually within 7 to 30 days. Some providers report within days, others take a few weeks.
- Can I take CE courses from other states to satisfy Illinois requirements? Only if those courses have specific IDOI approval for Illinois credit. Most out-of-state courses do not count unless specifically approved.
- Are webinar and live-streamed courses accepted? Yes, if they're IDOI-approved. For your 21 general CE hours, Illinois accepts online self-paced, webinar, and live classroom formats — for those hours, approval matters more than format. The one exception is the mandatory 3-hour ethics course: Illinois requires ethics CE to be taken as live classroom or live webinar instruction, so a self-paced online ethics course won't count toward the requirement (50 Ill. Adm. Code 3119.45). Confirm the format when you enroll in an ethics course.
- What's the best time to check for approved providers? Check before enrolling in any course, not after. Verifying approval after completing a non-approved course doesn't help — the hours won't count.
